Why Digemy

Polyplast's promise is precision-engineered plastic — every beer crate, basin and bucket leaving Douala must meet exact dimensional and structural spec. That promise is made or broken on the production floor, by operators who calibrate the machines, manage the moulds and catch defects before they ship.

Where the loss happens — an estimated $690K–$950K a year:

Resin Waste Goods Rejects Equipment Downtime Safety & Compliance Capacity Shortfall

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy maps and baselines knowledge across every Polyplast frontline role — Machine Operator, Mould Technician, Quality Inspector and Shift Supervisor. Spaced-reinforcement microlearning drives retention of extruder calibration protocol, mould changeover procedure and quality inspection standards. Real-time dashboards surface knowledge gaps by production line and shift, pinpointing exactly where Polyplast’s $690K–$950K in annual leakage is concentrating. Evaluation scorecards at individual, line and factory level create accountability — turning that leakage into $69K–$95K of recovered performance across the full production operation.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Resin & Material Waste
Extruder operators without reinforced knowledge of temperature profiles, material blend ratios and die settings consume 2–3% more resin than optimised production requires. At Polyplast's scale, that invisible overspend compounds to hundreds of thousands of dollars annually before it ever shows up on a report.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2%
Annual Leakage$150K–$200K
Recovery @10%$15K–$20K
Effects
Resin cost inflation of 2–3% compresses margins on every production run; melt inconsistency produces brittle or out-of-spec products that either fail QC or reach customers as substandard. Scrap volumes rise and raw material purchasing pressure intensifies.
The Digemy Difference
Microlearning modules on extruder calibration, material ratio protocols and blend sequencing — reinforced by role-specific dashboards that flag waste deviation by shift and operator, so supervisors catch drift before it becomes a batch loss.
Leakage 02
Finished Goods Rejects
Mould tolerance, cooling time and surface inspection are precision skills. Operators without reinforced knowledge miss dimensional deviations and structural weaknesses before products ship — triggering returns, rework and credit notes, particularly from anchor customer UCB Brewery where a single defective crate batch carries significant commercial cost.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5%
Annual Leakage$150K–$225K
Recovery @10%$15K–$22.5K
Effects
Customer returns and replacement costs; rework labour and material expense; reputational exposure with UCB Brewery and other key accounts that drives long-term volume risk well beyond the immediate financial hit.
The Digemy Difference
Quality inspection scorecards and mould-setting protocols trained and tracked by operator and production line — with individual accountability for defect rates visible to supervisors in real time, not surfaced weeks later in a quality audit.
Leakage 03
Unplanned Equipment Downtime
Plastic extrusion equipment is sensitive to operator procedure. Incorrect start-up sequences, failure to identify early warning signs and reactive maintenance habits cause unplanned stoppages that idle production lines worth thousands of dollars per shift.
Loss Ratio0.5%–0.75%
Annual Leakage$60K–$90K
Recovery @10%$6K–$9K
Effects
Lost throughput on high-capacity extrusion lines; emergency maintenance costs that dwarf what planned maintenance would have cost; order fulfilment delays that strain customer relationships.
The Digemy Difference
Machine operator knowledge baselines covering correct start-up, shutdown and fault identification — with dashboard visibility of knowledge gaps by operator and shift, so maintenance investment targets the highest-risk individuals before the next stoppage.
Leakage 04
Safety & Regulatory Incidents
Plastics extrusion involves high heat, pressurised equipment and chemical exposure. Operators without reinforced safety knowledge incur higher rates of lost-time injuries — creating CNPS liability, lost production capacity and the compounding cost of reactive safety management rather than prevention.
Loss Ratio2%–4% of payroll
Annual Leakage$30K–$60K
Recovery @10%$3K–$6K
Effects
Lost-time injuries and CNPS penalty exposure; elevated insurance premiums; regulatory scrutiny that creates business continuity risk and management distraction that compounds production inefficiency.
The Digemy Difference
Safety protocol microlearning with CNPS-aligned compliance tracking and individual scorecard accountability — shifting Polyplast from reactive incident response to proactive, measurable safety performance.
Leakage 05
New Line Capacity Shortfall
Polyplast's investment in new machinery is a growth bet — but new equipment only delivers its capacity promise when operators can run it at full spec. Knowledge gaps in new machine procedures, throughput optimisation and quality standards delay payback on that capital investment, month after month.
Loss Ratio20%–25%
Annual Leakage$300K–$375K
Recovery @10%$30K–$37.5K
Effects
Extended ramp-up periods on newly installed machinery; sub-optimal yield from equipment running below rated capacity; delayed return on capital investment that undermines the financial case for continued expansion.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific onboarding for new equipment with spaced reinforcement of machine specs, SOPs and throughput targets — building the operator competency needed to hit full capacity within 90 days of line commissioning, not 12 months.
