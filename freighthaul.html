Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Driver Non-Compliance & Incident Costs
Long-haul drivers who aren't consistently reinforced on hours-of-service rules, RTMS standards, and load-securing protocols expose Freighthaul to regulatory fines, insurance excess payments, and fleet downtime — costs that compound across a six-province operation. Each serious incident carries direct financial liability plus the reputational risk of a client whose cargo didn't arrive intact.
Loss Ratio1.5%–3%
Annual LeakageR1.80m–R3.60m
Recovery @10%R180k–R360k
Effects
Regulatory fines, insurance excess costs, and vehicle downtime eat directly into margin. Repeat incidents trigger client SLA breaches and premium increases that erode competitiveness on contested freight lanes.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys short compliance modules — refreshed by spaced repetition — so every driver stays current on RTMS, fatigue management, and load-securing rules. Branch-level dashboards flag which drivers are falling behind before the incident happens.
Leakage 02
Subcontractor Induction & Quality Failures
Freighthaul's subcontractor network is a strategic capacity asset — but when sub-contractors aren't properly inducted on client SOPs and cargo standards, the brand risk and claims liability fall squarely on Freighthaul. Informal verbal briefings are not sufficient when cargo values run to hundreds of thousands of rand per load.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR1.20m–R2.40m
Recovery @10%R120k–R240k
Effects
Cargo claims, client disputes, and damaged-goods write-offs accumulate from subcontractors who weren't briefed to Freighthaul's standard. A single high-value FMCG or automotive load mishandled can wipe out the margin on an entire lane for a month.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy's platform extends structured induction to subcontractor drivers via mobile — ensuring consistent briefing on client SOPs, handling requirements, and documentation standards regardless of whether the driver is on the core fleet or not.
Leakage 03
Fuel Inefficiency from Untrained Driver Behaviour
Fuel accounts for roughly 30% of long-haul road freight operating costs — making driver behaviour the single largest controllable variable in the cost structure. Harsh braking, excessive idling, poor gear discipline, and non-optimal speed management can waste 10–15% of the fuel pool annually, representing R3.6m–R5.4m in recoverable cost at Freighthaul's estimated scale.
Loss Ratio10%–15% of fuel pool
Annual LeakageR3.60m–R5.40m
Recovery @10%R360k–R540k
Effects
Poor fuel discipline inflates cost-per-km, erodes per-lane profitability, and accelerates engine and drivetrain wear — increasing maintenance cost alongside fuel spend. On long-haul corridors, the cumulative impact compounds across hundreds of trips per year.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces eco-driving principles through short, repeatable modules — so fuel-smart behaviour becomes habit, not a once-off induction. Tracking data from Freighthaul's on-board telematics can benchmark improvement over time.
Leakage 04
POD & Billing Documentation Errors
When drivers and coordinators aren't well-trained on proof-of-delivery procedures and billing documentation standards, invoices get disputed, revenue gets delayed, and some accounts are written off entirely. Across a multi-branch operation with hundreds of loads per month, small documentation failures add up fast.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR0.60m–R1.80m
Recovery @10%R60k–R180k
Effects
Incomplete or incorrectly captured PODs delay cash collection, trigger client disputes, and create write-off exposure on invoices that can't be substantiated. Finance teams spend disproportionate time chasing documentation instead of closing books.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces POD and documentation standards across drivers and coordinators through role-specific modules — with evaluation scorecards that identify which teams or individuals are creating the most billing risk before it reaches the client.
Leakage 05
Client Churn from Service-Level Failures
In long-haul freight, clients award lanes based on trust — and trust is destroyed by repeated service failures: wrong collections, late deliveries, damaged goods, or poor communication during exceptions. Frontline teams that haven't been trained on client SOPs and communication protocols put long-term contract revenue at risk on every trip.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR1.20m–R2.40m
Recovery @10%R120k–R240k
Effects
Service failures cascade: a single FMCG or automotive client lost to a competitor represents not just one lane but the entire relationship. In a market where freight rates are contested and margins are thin, client retention is a direct profit lever.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy equips Freighthaul's customer-facing teams with client-specific SOP knowledge and exception-management skills — building the service consistency that turns satisfied clients into long-term, multi-lane partners.
