Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Sales Conversion Failure
Outbound telesales is Clientèle’s primary new business engine, yet inconsistent product knowledge and objection-handling gaps drive conversion rates below their potential. Every percentage point of conversion lost directly erases premium from the in-force book.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR20m–R30m
Recovery @10%R2m–R3m
Effects
Lower agent confidence shortens calls and leaves more objections unresolved. Quota shortfalls at team level create pressure to rush FAIS disclosure — compounding the compliance risk alongside the revenue risk.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific microlearning on product features, FAIS disclosure sequences and objection-handling frameworks ensures agents respond with precision on every call — not just in the first week after induction.
Leakage 02
Lapse & Debit Order Attrition
Clientèle’s own 2025 annual results flag high levels of withdrawals, suspended debit order mandates and disputes as a key earnings headwind. Retention agents under-equipped on DebiCheck processes and hardship de-escalation fail to protect in-force premium that is costly to replace.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2%
Annual LeakageR30m–R40m
Recovery @10%R3m–R4m
Effects
Lapses are permanent — each lost policy exits the book and depresses the contractual service margin release. Untrained retention agents escalate disputes rather than resolve them, accelerating the churn cycle.
The Digemy Difference
Retention-specific training tracks covering DebiCheck resolution, financial hardship objection scripts and FSCA-compliant reinstatement protocols keep agents sharp on the competencies that directly protect in-force premium.
Leakage 03
Product Penetration Failure
Clientèle offers one of South Africa’s broadest direct-to-consumer financial services suites — life, disability, funeral, legal, investment, mobile and rewards — yet cross-sell rates remain low when agents are trained only on their primary product. Each single-product client is both a revenue gap and a higher lapse risk.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR10m–R20m
Recovery @10%R1m–R2m
Effects
Single-product clients lapse at higher rates, reducing retention performance. Low cross-sell commission limits agent earnings and raises sales force turnover — feeding back into conversion and ramp-cost leakage.
The Digemy Difference
Cross-product microlearning paths expose agents to the full Clientèle suite — from Legal Protect to CBC Rewards — enabling them to identify client needs and present relevant products at every touchpoint without requiring separate programmes.
Leakage 04
FAIS & Conduct Compliance Failures
The FSCA’s Treating Customers Fairly framework demands ongoing competency across Clientèle’s entire frontline. Agents who miss disclosure obligations, mis-frame product risks or fail to evidence client understanding expose the business to policy recissions, regulatory sanctions and reputational damage.
Loss RatioEstimated
Annual LeakageR5m–R10m
Recovery @10%R500k–R1m
Effects
A single FSCA conduct finding can trigger audit of the full sales book. Policy recissions erode both in-force premium and the contractual service margin, with systemic non-compliance penalties running into multiples of the original leakage figure.
The Digemy Difference
Always-on FAIS compliance microlearning — updated in real time with FSCA circulars and tied to individual role profiles — keeps every agent current on disclosure rules, TCF obligations and product risk framing without disrupting production targets.
Leakage 05
Field Agent Ramp & Attrition
Clientèle’s field force carries disproportionate new business weight in regions where call-centre reach is limited. High field agent attrition — typical of incentive-heavy direct insurance distribution — and slow ramp to full productivity create a recurring premium gap that in-force growth cannot easily absorb.
Loss Ratio0.8%–1.2%
Annual LeakageR16m–R24m
Recovery @10%R1.6m–R2.4m
Effects
New agents in their first six months produce a fraction of an experienced agent’s premium output. Every departing field agent takes existing-client relationships with them, accelerating lapse risk in those accounts and resetting the ramp cost clock.
The Digemy Difference
Mobile-first onboarding and role-specific learning pipelines cut field agent time-to-productivity by up to 75%. Gamification, progress tracking and milestone rewards reduce early attrition — converting ramp cost into sustained production.
