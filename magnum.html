Why Digemy

Magnum built its training backbone inside Unilever — skills matrices, WSP submissions, compliance calendars, all inherited. Since separating in December 2025, the team has focused on keeping that compliance engine running while a 170-person frontline across the Lordsview factory, Sandton and Durban learns to operate as its own company. The gap isn't compliance — it's everyday capability: the cold-chain discipline, changeover accuracy and onboarding speed that don't show up on an audit but show up in spoilage, rework and ramp time.

“Every missed freezer check is a tonne of ice cream that never reaches a shelf.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy layers spaced-repetition reinforcement on top of Magnum's existing skills matrix — nothing is rebuilt, nothing is replaced. Cold-chain protocols, changeover steps and role-specific onboarding become 1–2 minute daily recaps, sent via QR code or WhatsApp, so the frontline retains what compliance training already covers instead of forgetting it between sessions. Every completion also auto-feeds WSP and B-BBEE reporting, turning existing training spend into evidence rather than paperwork.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Cold Storage & Temperature Discipline
Spoilage and melt loss trace back to human process, not equipment: missed freezer checks, slow reaction to temperature drift, and inconsistent handling during changeovers. In a cold-chain business, this is the single biggest lever on the floor.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR3.0m–R6.0m
Recovery @10%R300k–R600k
Effects
Product write-offs, wasted raw material and energy, and inconsistent quality reaching retail — all invisible until stock reconciliation.
The Digemy Difference
Daily micro-recaps on temperature thresholds and freezer discipline, reinforced until the behaviour is automatic — not a once-off induction module that fades within weeks.
Leakage 02
SKU Changeover & Calibration Errors
Different SKUs (minis vs. impulses) need different machine parameters. Operators who don't fully retain the calibration steps for every changeover cause line stoppages, off-spec product and rework.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R150k–R300k
Effects
Line downtime during changeovers, off-spec batches, and repeated re-training on the same calibration steps every time a new SKU cycle starts.
The Digemy Difference
Scenario-based recaps tied to each SKU family, triggered ahead of scheduled changeovers so the right steps are top of mind before the line stops, not after.
Leakage 03
New Hire Ramp & Onboarding Backlog
Job descriptions and skills matrices inherited from Unilever are still being validated against how Magnum actually runs today. New hires are trained against content that may not fully match current process, slowing ramp and raising early-tenure error rates.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R4.5m
Recovery @10%R150k–R450k
Effects
Slower time-to-competence for new operators, higher supervisor time spent on manual correction, and inconsistent onboarding across shifts and sites.
The Digemy Difference
Existing SOPs, skills matrices and recorded training sessions convert into short, role-specific microlearning frameworks — cutting build time from months to days and ramp time by up to 30%.
Leakage 04
Multi-Shift Knowledge Consistency
The factory runs 24 hours across multiple shifts. Without a shared, reinforced knowledge base, each shift develops its own shortcuts and workarounds — some safe, some not — with no easy way for supervisors to see where the gaps are.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R150k–R300k
Effects
Process drift between shifts, inconsistent quality outcomes depending on who's on the line, and no shared visibility into which shift needs support.
The Digemy Difference
One reinforced knowledge base across all shifts, with manager dashboards showing exactly where each team and each individual sits on retention and accuracy.
Leakage 05
Frontline Engagement & Compliance Reporting
Frontline staff who joined under Unilever are still building an identity around the new, standalone Magnum. Low engagement in that transition compounds every other gap on this list — and manual completion tracking makes WSP and B-BBEE reporting harder than it needs to be.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R150k–R300k
Effects
Lower training uptake, manual chasing of completion records, and missed opportunity to reinforce a new company culture through daily touchpoints.
The Digemy Difference
Gamified, white-labelled learning under the Magnum brand — with automated WSP-ready completion records and B-BBEE-aligned reporting built in, not bolted on.
