Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Upsell & Combo Conversion Failure
Crew who haven't internalised suggestive selling leave R8–R15 of transaction value unclaimed with every interaction. Across 100 stores and millions of annual transactions, missed upsells compound into the single largest recoverable revenue gap in the business.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.5%
Annual LeakageR18m–R30m
Recovery @10%R1.8m–R3.0m
Effects
Basket values sit below brand benchmark, promotional attachment rates underperform, and the investment in digital kiosks is undermined when crew can't reinforce the upsell face-to-face. Average transaction yield drifts below comparable franchise groups.
The Digemy Difference
Spaced reinforcement on suggestive selling scripts, combo call-outs, and digital kiosk bridge training — delivered in daily 5-minute modules with manager accountability dashboards that flag stores where attachment rates are slipping.
Leakage 02
Food Waste from Portioning & Production Errors
Inaccurate portioning inflates COGS and produces inconsistent product quality across stores. Overproduction during peak hours generates discard waste that directly erodes margin on every shift — a training failure often misread as a supply chain problem.
Loss Ratio0.7%–1.2%
Annual LeakageR8.4m–R14.4m
Recovery @10%R0.8m–R1.4m
Effects
COGS drift above target store by store, brand auditors flag product inconsistency across locations, and waste reporting data obscures the real root cause — knowledge decay between induction and the daily reality of a busy lunch rush.
The Digemy Difference
Visual portioning standards and waste-control SOPs embedded via microlearning — reinforced daily so crew retain correct prep procedures even under peak throughput pressure, with supervisor check-ins triggered automatically at 30-day intervals.
Leakage 03
New Store Ramp-Up Underperformance
Each new Rohloff store takes 4–8 months to reach full throughput as crew master service flows, menu knowledge, and system processes. With 8+ new stores opening per year and a stated ambition to double the estate, slow ramp is a structural and compounding drag on revenue.
Loss Ratio15%–25% shortfall
Annual LeakageR6.0m–R10.0m
Recovery @10%R0.6m–R1.0m
Effects
New store RGMs absorb disproportionate coaching time re-training crew from scratch, early guest experience scores underperform brand benchmarks, and the support burden flows back onto the RSC team in Somerset West.
The Digemy Difference
Pre-opening learning journeys and structured 90-day new store induction paths compress ramp time materially — Digemy's benchmark with FLM Academy: 70% faster time-to-competence, applied here to every Rohloff new-store opening.
Leakage 04
CHAMPS Compliance Failures
Yum! Brands holds franchisees to rigorous CHAMPS standards across Cleanliness, Hospitality, Accuracy, Maintenance, Product quality, and Speed. Knowledge gaps in frontline execution trigger audit failures, remediation costs, and — at scale — brand relationship risk that no franchise can afford to ignore.

Annual LeakageR3.6m–R6.0m
Recovery @10%R0.4m–R0.6m
Effects
Below-threshold CHAMPS scores trigger re-audits and RSC escalations, consuming management time better spent on growth. Guest trust erodes in affected stores, and the hard-won Franchise Partner of the Year reputation is exposed to store-level inconsistency.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific CHAMPS modules — delivered on spaced repetition schedules — ensure standards stay embedded between audit cycles rather than surfacing as a last-minute recall exercise in the week before an inspector arrives.
Leakage 05
Team Turnover Cost (Training-Attributable)
QSR turnover typically runs at 60–90% annually. For Rohloff's ~2,800-strong frontline, that translates to nearly 2,000 exits per year — a quarter of which are preventable with stronger onboarding, early-tenure engagement, and visible career progression tools.

The Digemy Difference
Structured 30-60-90 day onboarding paths with automated check-in nudges and manager visibility dashboards reduce early-tenure exits — turning Rohloff's culture-led employer brand into a measurable retention advantage backed by data.
