Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Forecourt Upsell Failure
Forecourt attendants are MDH Group’s most frequent customer touchpoint — present at every fill-up across all KZN sites. Without consistent product knowledge training, they fail to offer lubricants, additives and wiper fluid at the pump, leaving the industry’s highest-margin upsell opportunity unaddressed on every transaction.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5% fuel rev
Annual LeakageR4.0m–R6.0m
Recovery @10%R400k–R600k
Effects
Missed lubricant and additive offers reduce gross margin per transaction and eliminate purchases customers would have made if prompted. Across high-volume KZN sites, this accumulates into R4m–R6m in revenue the forecourt crew leaves at the pump annually.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers daily bite-sized modules on lubricant grades, additive benefits and upsell timing — ensuring every attendant knows what to offer, when to offer it, and how to handle a decline. Knowledge is reinforced automatically so it stays sharp across seasons and staff changes.
Leakage 02
Convenience Store Conversion
Nearly half of all forecourt visitors are not buying fuel — they are buying food, beverages and groceries. Shop assistants who lack suggestive selling skills and promotional knowledge fail to grow basket sizes beyond the single item a customer already intended to purchase.
Loss Ratio1%–2% conv. rev
Annual LeakageR1.0m–R2.0m
Recovery @10%R100k–R200k
Effects
Undertrained shop staff allow customers to complete single-item transactions when a well-timed prompt — a meal deal, a linked promotion, a hot food pairing — could double basket value. The combined loss across MDH’s convenience operations is estimated at R1m–R2m annually.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces suggestive selling scripts, promotional bundle awareness and hot food pairing techniques in daily 2-minute modules — so shop assistants arrive for every shift knowing exactly what to offer and why.
Leakage 03
QSR & Food Service Quality
MDH Group’s forecourt QSR and coffee operations depend on consistent recipe execution and food safety compliance to protect customer experience and regulatory standing. Crew members who have not retained food prep standards introduce waste, inconsistency and hygiene exposure across the network.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.5% QSR rev
Annual LeakageR0.6m–R1.0m
Recovery @10%R60k–R100k
Effects
Recipe deviations increase raw material wastage and undermine the quality standard that drives repeat visits to destination forecourts. Food safety non-compliance introduces health risk and, in a regulated sector, the potential for closures and reputational damage.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds and reinforces HACCP basics, recipe specifications and service speed standards through spaced-reinforcement modules — converting once-off induction training into deep crew habit that holds up under peak-hour pressure.
Leakage 04
Shrinkage & Cash Discrepancies
High transaction volumes and multi-site operations create significant exposure to cash-handling errors, till discrepancies and stock shrinkage at MDH Group’s convenience counters. Cashiers who have not retained correct POS procedures and loss-prevention awareness are the primary point of failure.
Loss Ratio0.5%–0.8% conv. rev
Annual LeakageR0.5m–R0.8m
Recovery @10%R50k–R80k
Effects
POS discrepancies and unauthorized voids accumulate undetected in high-volume forecourt environments, often absorbed as operating cost rather than attributed to training failure. Across multiple KZN sites, even small per-transaction leakage compounds into hundreds of thousands annually.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces cash-handling procedures, void authorization protocols and basic loss-prevention awareness — closing the knowledge gap that allows small discrepancies to persist shift after shift without correction or detection.
Leakage 05
Brand Standards & Compliance Failures
MDH Group’s fuel retail sites operate under oil company dealer agreements that include regular brand audits, forecourt presentation requirements and safety protocols. Staff who have not retained these standards create compliance risk that affects audit outcomes, dealer relationships and renewal terms.
Loss RatioRisk-adjusted
Annual LeakageR0.7m–R1.2m
Recovery @10%R70k–R120k
Effects
Failed brand audits consume management time, invite remediation cost and weaken the group’s standing with oil company partners. In a regulated forecourt environment, safety and presentation compliance is non-negotiable — and knowledge gaps are the most common underlying cause.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy maps oil company brand standards, safety protocols and forecourt presentation requirements by role — then reinforces them through daily microlearning so compliance is a habit, not a pre-audit scramble.
