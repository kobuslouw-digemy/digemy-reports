Top 7 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Field Sales Conversion Gap
Montego’s field sales reps call on hundreds of independent pet shops, farm co-ops and hypermarket buyers. Without consistent product knowledge and objection-handling frameworks, reps default to order-taking rather than active selling — losing basket expansion and ranging opportunities on every call.
Loss Ratio1.6%–3%
Annual LeakageR18.4m–R34.5m
Recovery @10%R1.84m–R3.45m
Effects
Low conversion rates on new range introductions; competitor products winning shelf and counter space. Reps unable to articulate Montego’s premium differentiation under buyer scrutiny, defaulting to margin-eroding discounts.
The Digemy Difference
Mobile microlearning delivers range briefs, objection scripts and call-cycle coaching in under three minutes. Reinforced between call cycles, reps arrive at every door prepared — not improvising.
Leakage 02
Merchandising & Shelf Compliance Failures
Shelf presence is Montego’s first moment of truth in retail. Planogram non-compliance, poor POSM placement and out-of-stock blind spots in large-format stores compound daily into missed basket conversions that never show up on any single sales report.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR6.9m–R13.8m
Recovery @10%R0.69m–R1.38m
Effects
Competitor facings dominate at eye-line while Montego’s premium SKUs sit below the sightline. Branded POSM removed or ignored by store staff — Montego’s shelf investment evaporates before the customer arrives.
The Digemy Difference
Short visual compliance modules with image-check reinforcement ensure every merchandiser knows the planogram standard and can spot deviations on sight. Spaced follow-up reinforces shelf discipline as a direct revenue driver — not an administrative box-tick.
Leakage 03
Trade Partner Advocacy Gap
Independent pet shop staff are Montego’s highest-leverage sales channel — a single confident recommendation at the counter converts a trial buyer into a lifetime customer. Without structured product knowledge, staff default to recommending whatever brand they sold most of last week.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR13.8m–R27.6m
Recovery @10%R1.38m–R2.76m
Effects
First-time pet owners guided toward competitor brands at the most influential moment in their purchasing journey. Montego’s life-stage and specialised nutrition ranges never surfaced — premium product margin left on the counter.
The Digemy Difference
Trade partner microlearning — delivered via WhatsApp or mobile — builds Montego expertise at the point of recommendation. Counter staff become brand advocates through short daily engagements, with no classroom time required.
Leakage 04
Vet & Professional Channel Underperformance
Vets and vet nurses are among the most trusted voices in the SA pet food decision. Montego’s therapeutic and prescription ranges compete directly with imported premium brands in this channel — but vet clinic staff often lack the clinical feeding protocol knowledge needed to recommend Montego with confidence.

Annual LeakageR3.45m–R5.18m
Recovery @10%R0.35m–R0.52m
Effects
Vet recommendations default to established imported brands with more clinical familiarity. Montego’s clinical ranges remain under-indexed in vet-initiated purchases — trial packs expire without recommendation follow-through.
The Digemy Difference
Concise clinical product modules — built for busy vet staff — deliver feeding indication summaries, ingredient differentiation and recommendation confidence. Spaced reinforcement ensures product knowledge is retained between clinical refresher events.
Leakage 05
NPD Launch Adoption Shortfall
Montego launches new SKUs across multiple categories every year, but launch energy dissipates within weeks when field and trade teams haven’t internalised the product story. The result is slow ranging adoption, missed early trial windows and NPD revenue that never reaches its first-year potential.

Annual LeakageR36.8m–R55.2m
Recovery @10%R3.68m–R5.52m
Effects
Reps default to selling existing range instead of leading with NPD — launch windows close before the product builds traction. Trade partners unable to answer customer questions about new products, creating confusion at the shelf.
The Digemy Difference
AI-generated launch briefing modules are live within days of NPD sign-off — field reps and trade partners receive the full product story before the product hits the shelf. Launch energy is sustained through spaced reinforcement across the first 90 days of a product’s life.
Leakage 06
Production SOP Non-Compliance & Yield Loss
Montego’s manufacturing operations depend on precise formulation, extrusion tolerances and coating specifications across every batch. When operators don’t have those standards embedded — not just trained once — SOP deviations compound into yield loss, product rework and batch rejections that hit margin directly.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5% COGS
Annual LeakageR5.75m–R17.25m
Recovery @10%R0.58m–R1.73m
Effects
Ingredient wastage from mis-formulated batches; rework cycles that reduce throughput and inflate unit cost. Quality inconsistencies that trigger retailer returns and erode the product integrity Montego’s premium positioning depends on.
The Digemy Difference
Production microlearning delivers formulation specs, extrusion tolerances and quality checkpoint protocols in short daily reinforcements — so operators retain standards between formal training events and shift changes. Gap dashboards identify which line teams and shift patterns carry the highest deviation risk.
Leakage 07
Logistics & Distribution Knowledge Gaps
Pet food is a high-volume, weight-sensitive category where incorrect stacking, FIFO failures and cold chain breaks translate directly into damaged stock, expired returns and delivery errors. Distribution teams operating without embedded procedural knowledge create margin erosion that never appears on a single report.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1% of product value
Annual LeakageR9.2m–R18.4m
Recovery @10%R0.92m–R1.84m
Effects
Damaged product from incorrect pallet stacking and load weight violations; expired stock from FIFO non-compliance; cold chain breaks on premium wet and prescription ranges generating costly returns. Delivery errors erode retailer trust and create administrative recovery costs.
The Digemy Difference
Short procedural microlearning — covering stacking protocols, temperature monitoring, FIFO disciplines and delivery verification — is reinforced at shift level so knowledge is active at the point of action, not just on induction day.
