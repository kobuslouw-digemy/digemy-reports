Why Digemy

Mica's 160+ member stores trade on a single brand promise — knowledgeable, helpful hardware expertise delivered at the counter. But across a voluntary franchise network, knowledge depth varies dramatically from store to store, driving inconsistent customer experiences, basket under-capture, and avoidable product returns. Digemy closes that gap systematically: standardising product knowledge across the network, reinforcing it through spaced repetition, and making compliance and capability visible at store and individual level in real time.

“In a franchise network, every unanswered product question at your counter is a sale your nearest competitor just made.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy builds durable product expertise through scientifically-backed spaced reinforcement, ensuring technical knowledge acquired in training is retained on the shop floor where it drives margin. Management dashboards give the Mica franchise support team real-time visibility into knowledge gaps by store, by role and by product category — identifying your weakest links before they cost you customers. Evaluation scorecards at individual, store and network level create the accountability structures that translate improved knowledge into measurable revenue recovery across every member of the Mica network.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Technical Sales Conversion Gap
Mica's competitive advantage is expert advice — but when counter staff can't confidently answer technical product questions, customers walk out empty-handed or choose a cheaper, lower-margin product rather than the right one. Across 160+ stores, even a modest conversion shortfall compounds into millions of rands of lost network revenue annually.
Loss Ratio1.0%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR15.0m–R22.5m
Recovery @10%R1.5m–R2.3m
Effects
Customers leave without purchasing, switch to competitors, or buy an inferior product — reducing basket value and eroding the brand's "DIY Experts" positioning. Repeat visits decline as trust in staff expertise falters.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers bite-sized product knowledge modules — material compatibility, application techniques, safety specs — reinforced through spaced repetition so counter staff retain what they learn and convert more technical queries into confident sales.
Leakage 02
Paint Counter Errors & Returns
The paint counter is one of Mica's highest-margin and most technically demanding service points. Incorrect colour matching, wrong primer-substrate pairings and poor sheen-level guidance result in costly returns, rework and customer frustration that directly erodes the paint category's contribution margin.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR9.0m–R12.0m
Recovery @10%R900k–R1.2m
Effects
Returned and re-mixed paint stock, customer complaints and replacement costs accumulate quietly but significantly across a network carrying major paint brands like Plascon, Dulux and Duram. Supplier relationships and brand reputation are both at risk.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy's scenario-based microlearning trains paint specialists on mixing ratios, substrate compatibility and colour science — with retention quizzes and scorecards that ensure accuracy is maintained long after the initial training day.
Leakage 03
Basket Under-capture (Upsell Failure)
A customer buying a drill doesn't automatically think to add drill bits, cutting discs, safety goggles and a carry case — but a knowledgeable Mica advisor should. Across the network, the failure to systematically offer complementary products leaves significant basket value on the counter every single day.
Loss Ratio0.8%–1.2%
Annual LeakageR10.0m–R15.0m
Recovery @10%R1.0m–R1.5m
Effects
Revenue per transaction stays at entry-level while margin-accretive accessories and consumables go unpromoted. The network's average basket value underperforms its potential, compressing profitability per customer visit across all 160+ stores.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds contextual upselling frameworks into staff training — product pairings, project-based bundles and natural conversation prompts that staff can apply confidently at the counter without feeling like they're selling.
Leakage 04
Franchise Standards Non-Compliance
Mica's voluntary trading model empowers franchisees with autonomy — but it also creates variation in how brand standards, merchandising layouts, promotional execution and product training are applied. Stores that don't consistently implement network programmes dilute the Mica brand and leave revenue on the shelf.
Loss Ratio0.6%–0.8%
Annual LeakageR15.0m–R20.0m
Recovery @10%R1.5m–R2.0m
Effects
Inconsistent in-store experience erodes the brand's "DIY Experts" promise at the store level, and poor promotional execution means supplier co-op funding and promotional stock fail to convert into planned sales uplifts across the member base.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy's management dashboards give the Mica franchise support team real-time visibility into compliance adherence and knowledge levels by store — enabling targeted coaching interventions before non-compliance becomes a revenue problem.
Leakage 05
Seasonal Activation Failure
Mica's categories are acutely seasonal — garden and lawn in spring, pool care in summer, braai and outdoor living year-round, heating and waterproofing in winter. When staff aren't fluent in seasonal product ranges, promotional mechanics and activation timing, peak revenue windows close without being fully captured.

Effects
Seasonal stock sits longer than planned, promotional mechanics go unexplained at point of sale, and markdown pressure builds as windows close. Garden, pool and outdoor categories — structurally high-margin — underperform their seasonal revenue potential.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers push-notification microlearning ahead of every seasonal cycle — product refreshers, promotional briefs and activation scripts that ensure every Mica store activates its seasonal ranges at the same standard, on time, every time.
