Leakage 01
Commercial Fuels Sales Knowledge Gap

Sales representatives who cannot differentiate fuel grades, cite efficiency data, or handle competitor objections with confidence lose margin — and contracts — they should win.

Effects
Reps discount to close deals where product knowledge would have been sufficient, compressing margin on won contracts. Tenders are lost at the final stage to TotalEnergies, BP, and Engen teams who can articulate a stronger technical case.

Digemy deploys spaced product spec modules and competitive battlecards to commercial reps, refreshed quarterly and tied to deal-stage milestones. Manager dashboards flag knowledge decay before it hits the pipeline.
Leakage 02
Forecourt Upsell & Activation Failure
Across 850+ Astron Energy retail sites, every forecourt interaction is an opportunity to convert a pump transaction into a premium grade upgrade, lubricant recommendation, or loyalty enrolment. Undertrained attendants default to transaction mode, leaving incremental revenue on the forecourt at scale.

Annual LeakageR90m–R180m

Premium grade conversion stays low, diluting per-litre fuel margin across the network. Lubricant sales migrate to workshop competitors; loyalty under-enrolment weakens long-term customer retention.

Procedural knowledge gaps — particularly in high-turnover operational roles — are the primary driver of compliance events and their compounding financial and reputational costs.
Events p.a.2–4 events
