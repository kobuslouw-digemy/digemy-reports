Why Digemy

Evohealth manufactures and distributes across pharmaceuticals, medical devices, and consumer health lines to more than 300 public healthcare institutions and 1,500 retail pharmacies, which means every rep, warehouse operator, and QC technician represents a wider and more technical portfolio than most single-category competitors. That breadth is a commercial strength, but it multiplies the number of knowledge gaps that can quietly cost formulary placements, batch releases, and repeat orders. Digemy gives Evohealth's existing training function a way to keep every one of those roles current without slowing the pace of new product launches.

“Every rep who can't answer a pharmacist's second question is a formulary Evohealth just lost to someone who could.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy layers spaced-repetition refreshers onto Evohealth's existing induction and product-training material, closing the gap between what's launched and what the field actually retains. Retention data lets the training team see which SKUs, depots, or rep cohorts need reinforcement before a launch quarter closes — turning training from a once-off event into a measurable, ongoing recovery lever.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Sales Rep Clinical & Product Knowledge Gaps
Evohealth's field team sells across pharmaceuticals, devices, and consumer health lines, and pharmacists and hospital buyers routinely test reps on cross-category detail. When a rep can't answer confidently, the account defaults to a single-category competitor it already trusts — a switch that's rarely reversed inside the same financial year.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR1.65m–R3.3m
Recovery @10%R165k–R330k
Effects
Formulary and shelf-space decisions go to competitors with narrower but more confidently pitched ranges. Reps spend follow-up visits re-earning trust instead of opening new accounts, extending sales cycles across the affected territory.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy refreshes product and clinical knowledge on a spaced schedule tied to Evohealth's actual SKU calendar, so reps stay sharp on the full portfolio rather than whatever they last revised. Managers get visibility into which reps or regions are due for reinforcement before the next call cycle.
Leakage 02
Warehouse & GDP Compliance Errors
Good Distribution Practice governs temperature control, batch traceability, and pick accuracy across Evohealth's warehouse operation, and a single mis-pick or cold-chain lapse can write off stock or trigger a compliance query. Turnover and shift patterns mean procedure knowledge decays faster than annual refreshers can catch.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.2%
Annual LeakageR1.4m–R3.36m
Recovery @10%R140k–R336k
Effects
Mis-picked or mishandled stock gets written off, returned, or held for investigation, and repeat compliance queries slow down SAHPRA and client audits. Each incident also pulls a supervisor off the floor to investigate.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps GDP and pick-accuracy protocols live through short, recurring checks rather than a once-a-year refresher, so procedure knowledge doesn't quietly lapse between audits. New starters reach full competence faster because their training follows the same spaced schedule as tenured staff.
Leakage 03
QA/QC Documentation & Deviation Gaps
Batch release depends on QC documentation being complete and consistent the first time; any deviation triggers rework, delayed release, or in the worst case a rejected batch. Evohealth's cGMP and SAHPRA obligations leave little room for a technician working from memory rather than current protocol.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR0.95m–R1.9m
Recovery @10%R95k–R190k
Effects
Delayed batch release pushes stock availability dates out, which cascades into missed pharmacy and tender delivery windows. Rework and re-testing also consume QC capacity that should be clearing the next batch in the queue.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy locks in documentation and deviation-handling protocols through spaced recall rather than a single onboarding session, so technicians retain the exact steps regulators expect. Evohealth's QA lead gets a clear view of where knowledge is fading before it shows up in an audit finding.
Leakage 04
Order Desk & Backorder Mismanagement
Order desk agents are the last checkpoint before an order ships, and errors in quantity, substitution, or backorder communication push pharmacies toward a supplier that gets it right the first time. Independent pharmacies in particular have low tolerance for repeated fulfilment friction.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR1.05m–R3.15m
Recovery @10%R105k–R315k
Effects
Incorrect or delayed orders generate credit notes, re-deliveries, and frustrated calls that eat agent capacity. Pharmacies that experience repeated errors quietly shift a growing share of their order volume elsewhere.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps order-processing and backorder-communication procedures fresh through regular spaced practice, cutting the error rate that drives churn. Evohealth can track which agents or product categories generate the most order errors and target reinforcement there.
Leakage 05
New Product Launch Adoption Shortfall
Each new SKU Evohealth launches carries a revenue potential the field team is expected to convert within the first few quarters, but reps trained only at launch briefing forget positioning detail well before that window closes. The shortfall shows up as an underperforming launch rather than a visible failure.
Loss Ratio20%–35%
Annual LeakageR4.4m–R7.7m
Recovery @10%R440k–R770k
Effects
New products miss their adoption curve, tying up working capital in slow-moving stock and pushing back the payback period on registration and manufacturing investment. Sales leadership often only spots the shortfall once a launch has already stalled.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy extends launch training past the initial briefing with spaced reinforcement through the critical adoption window, keeping positioning current as reps move on to the next launch. Retention data flags underperforming reps or regions early enough to intervene before the launch window closes.
