Leakage 01
Phlebotomy Errors & Patient Experience Failure
Haemolysis from poor venepuncture technique, wrong order-of-draw, and mislabelled tubes drive specimen rejection and costly re-collections across 300+ Care Centres. Each failed draw damages the patient relationship and erodes the referring doctor’s confidence in Ampath as a quality partner.

Annual LeakageR11.0m–R33.0m

Effects
Specimen rejection triggers repeat blood draws, delays results, and consumes lab technologist time on re-processing. Doctors who experience chronic turnaround failures migrate referrals to competing labs, compounding the revenue impact beyond the cost of re-collection.
The Digemy Difference
Spaced microlearning keeps Ampath’s phlebotomists sharp on venepuncture protocol, tube sequencing, and patient communication — reducing re-draw rates and protecting the referral relationships that anchor Ampath’s market share.
Leakage 02
Specimen Transport Chain Integrity
Drivers and couriers who lack sufficient knowledge of temperature stability windows, specimen transport timeframes, and biohazard packaging requirements cause sample degradation and test rejection before specimens reach the reference lab. This silent failure generates invisible rework costs and delayed results.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageR16.5m–R33.0m
Recovery @10%R1.65m–R3.3m
Effects
Degraded specimens require patient recalls, repeat phlebotomy appointments, and re-analysis — all at Ampath’s cost. Repeat occurrences at specific sites flag systemic knowledge gaps that are currently invisible to management.
The Digemy Difference
Targeted microlearning on cold-chain requirements, specimen stability by test type, and biohazard handling converts courier staff from the last point of failure into a quality assurance asset embedded across Ampath’s logistics network.
Leakage 03
Branch Admin Billing & Charge Capture Gaps
Incorrect ICD-10 coding, unbilled add-on tests, and incomplete patient medical aid information result in revenue that is processed but never collected. Across 300+ Care Centres processing thousands of patient visits daily, even marginal per-visit leakage aggregates into material annual loss.

Annual LeakageR16.5m–R49.5m

Effects
Under-billed tests and claim rejections from incorrect coding create write-offs that accumulate undetected at branch level. Medical aid clawbacks and billing disputes further erode net revenue and consume administrative bandwidth.
The Digemy Difference
Ongoing reinforcement of billing protocols, test panel codes, and medical aid submission rules ensures branch administrators capture every billable item — turning billing compliance from a compliance risk into a revenue recovery lever.

Medical technologists and lab scientists uncertain on Westgard QC rules, delta check thresholds, or critical value escalation chains introduce analytical errors and notification delays. For a laboratory underpinned by ISO 15189 accreditation, protocol drift at the bench is both a quality and an accreditation risk.
