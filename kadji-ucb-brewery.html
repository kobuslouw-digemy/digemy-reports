Why Digemy

UCB’s dominance as Cameroon’s largest locally-owned brewery is built on product excellence — a promise that lives or dies at the hands of the frontline operators, drivers, and sales teams who make it real every day. When knowledge of brewing standards, cold chain protocols, and on-trade activation requirements is inconsistent across the workforce, operational excellence erodes silently, converting into revenue leakage across every shift, every delivery, and every sales call.

Where the loss happens — an estimated XAF 2,700m–4,200m a year:

Channel Sales On-Trade Activation Cold Chain Quality Brewing Compliance NPD Adoption

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy maps and baselines knowledge across every UCB frontline role — Brewery Operator, Distribution Driver, Sales Representative and Brand Ambassador. Spaced-reinforcement microlearning drives retention of on-trade activation protocols, cold chain compliance standards and product portfolio knowledge across Kadji-Beer, K44 and the Spécial range. Real-time dashboards surface knowledge gaps by depot and territory, pinpointing exactly where UCB’s XAF 2,700m–4,200m in annual leakage is concentrating. Evaluation scorecards at individual, depot and network level create accountability — turning that leakage into XAF 270m–420m of recovered performance across UCB’s entire Cameroon footprint.

Leakage 02
On-Trade Activation Failures
Bars, restaurants, and nightclubs represent UCB’s highest-margin channel — and activation quality at these outlets determines brand visibility, product rotation, and sales velocity. Inconsistent knowledge of display standards, temperature requirements, and promotional mechanics costs UCB on its most profitable touchpoint.
Loss Ratio0.8%–1.2% on-trade
Annual LeakageXAF 400m–600m
Recovery @10%XAF 40m–60m
Effects
Warm product served at the bar, poor POS and visibility execution, missed promotional activation windows, and ambassadors unable to close on-trade listing conversations.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds on-trade activation knowledge into the daily routines of UCB ambassadors and reps. Outlet-level scorecards verify activation compliance and create direct accountability for the brand standards that defend UCB’s premium position.
Leakage 03
Cold Chain & Product Integrity
Kadji-Beer’s quality promise depends on the cold chain knowledge of every driver, distributor, and outlet operator across the delivery network. A single temperature deviation can compromise batch quality, generate returns, and damage UCB’s positioning in markets where product quality is the primary brand differentiator.
Loss Ratio0.5%–0.8%
Annual LeakageXAF 500m–800m
Recovery @10%XAF 50m–80m
Effects
Product returns from outlets due to temperature non-compliance, consumer quality complaints, and brand equity damage in on-trade accounts where UCB competes on quality differentiation against international competitors.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces cold chain protocols, product handling standards, and temperature compliance across the full distribution workforce. Assessments verify that knowledge is applied at every delivery point — not just received at training.

Brewing Quality & Compliance Failures
Multi-product brewing requires every operator to hold precise knowledge of parameters for Kadji-Beer, K44, Spécial soft drinks, and Madiba Water simultaneously. Procedural deviation — even minor — risks batch rejection, specification inconsistency, and ANOR regulatory non-compliance that carries reputational and commercial consequence.
