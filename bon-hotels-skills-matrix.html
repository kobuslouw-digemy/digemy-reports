How Digemy Closes the Gap
Spaced Reinforcement — Not Once-Off Training

Every skill in this matrix shares one critical vulnerability: it is taught once and forgotten quickly without consistent reinforcement. Induction programmes, printed SOPs and annual workshops do not change behaviour at scale — repeated, contextual microlearning does. Digemy's spaced repetition engine delivers 3-to-5-minute modules to staff phones on a schedule designed to maximise retention at the precise frequency the science supports. The skill required in the service moment is the skill the team actually has.
