Why Digemy

Carrol Boyes is a brand that lives or dies by its story — but across 24 stores and a nationwide wholesale network, the quality of that storytelling is only as strong as the consultant standing in front of the customer. When product knowledge is inconsistent, new collections sit untouched while consultants default to familiar best-sellers, corporate gifting proposals fall flat, and premium pricing becomes harder to justify. Digemy closes the gap between what consultants are trained on and what they actually retain and apply on the floor.

“In a brand built on storytelling, the moment your consultant can’t tell the story, you don’t just lose the sale — you lose the brand.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy embeds product and brand knowledge through spaced reinforcement, ensuring every consultant — in-store, corporate, or wholesale — can articulate the design heritage, new collection narratives, and gifting value proposition with consistency. The result: higher average basket values, faster new collection adoption, and a brand experience that matches the premium price point.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
New Collection Adoption Failure
Carrol Boyes launches multiple collections annually across 1,000+ SKUs, yet consultants overwhelmingly default to familiar best-sellers when engaging customers. New products sit at low velocity not because of demand failure, but because the people selling them don't know the design narrative well enough to champion them.
Loss Ratio25%–35%
Annual LeakageR7.5m–R10.5m
Recovery @10%R750k–R1.05m
Effects
New collections underperform against launch targets, generating margin pressure and excess inventory. Design and production investment in new lines yields diminishing returns when the sales team can't convert awareness into purchase.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers pre-launch microlearning on new collection narratives — design intent, material story, occasion positioning — so consultants arrive on launch day ready to sell with confidence. Spaced reinforcement ensures new product knowledge sticks beyond the first two weeks.
Leakage 02
In-Store Conversion & Upsell Failure
Carrol Boyes is a considered purchase — customers enter stores curious, not always decided. A consultant who can link products to lifestyle, occasion, or the brand's design heritage converts browsers into buyers and single items into curated sets. Without that depth, average basket value stays low and premium pricing becomes hard to defend.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR2.0m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R200k–R300k
Effects
Lower conversion rates, smaller average baskets, and customers lost to competitors who present a more compelling value story. Premium positioning erodes when consultants can't substantiate the price point with product knowledge.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces brand story modules, product category knowledge, and occasion-based selling techniques through bite-sized daily learning, ensuring consultant confidence stays high across the full product range — not just the top 20 sellers.
Leakage 03
Corporate & Gifting Sales Underperformance
Corporate gifting is a high-value channel where a single order can represent R50,000–R500,000. Consultants who don't know the full catalogue — or can't build a compelling bespoke gifting proposal — lose deals to competitors who can. Incomplete knowledge of product pairings, customisation options, and volume pricing undermines the pitch.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.5%
Annual LeakageR1.2m–R2.0m
Recovery @10%R120k–R200k
Effects
Lost corporate accounts, lower average deal values, and missed repeat gifting cycles from companies who would otherwise purchase annually. B2B clients who receive weak proposals don't return.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds catalogue mastery and consultative selling skills through role-specific learning paths for corporate gifting teams — covering product pairing logic, occasion mapping, and high-value proposal framing. Knowledge gaps that cost deals get closed before the next pitch.
Leakage 04
Trade Partner & Stockist Knowledge Gaps
Carrol Boyes products are stocked in 27+ countries across major retail partners including Bloomingdale's and David Jones. When trade partner staff can't articulate the brand story or differentiate Carrol Boyes from adjacent homeware brands, products are merchandised poorly, sales velocity drops, and reorder rates suffer.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR0.8m–R1.6m
Recovery @10%R80k–R160k
Effects
Underperforming stockist locations reduce wholesale reorder frequency and weaken the brand's premium positioning in multi-brand retail environments. Without brand knowledge, trade partners default to selling on price rather than design story.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys lightweight brand ambassador modules for trade partner staff — accessible on any device — covering design heritage, collection stories, and brand differentiation in under 10 minutes per session. Consistent brand knowledge drives consistent sell-through across the wholesale network.
Leakage 05
Product Returns & After-Care Knowledge Failure
Carrol Boyes products span multiple materials — pewter, stainless steel, ceramic, glass, leather — each with specific care, use, and compatibility requirements. When consultants fail to communicate this at point of sale, customers experience product failure or misuse, generating avoidable returns, warranty claims, and reputational damage to a brand built on quality.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.0m–R2.0m
Recovery @10%R100k–R200k
Effects
Avoidable returns generate stock writeoffs, reverse logistics costs, and negative reviews that undermine brand trust. Repeat gifting customers who receive items that tarnish or break prematurely are unlikely to return.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds material-specific after-care and compatibility knowledge into onboarding and ongoing reinforcement cycles, ensuring every consultant understands what to communicate at the point of sale — reducing returns and protecting the brand's quality promise.
