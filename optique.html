Why Digemy

Optique's mission — radical affordability, simplified access — is only as strong as the frontline team that delivers it. When optical dispensers can't fluently explain Essilor lens technology, when branch managers lack the tools to track recall rates by location, and when contact lens cross-selling is left to chance, the gap between the promise and the patient experience widens quietly across 22 branches.

Digemy closes that gap by embedding structured, role-specific knowledge into daily workflows — so every optometrist, dispenser, and receptionist knows exactly what to do, every time, and management can see where the gaps are before they become lost revenue.

“Eye care simplified only works when the team simplifying it knows the product inside out — and remembers it on a Tuesday at 4pm.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy maps and baselines knowledge across every frontline role — optometrist, dispenser, contact lens advisor, and branch manager. Spaced-reinforcement microlearning drives retention of lens technology, compliance protocols, and conversion techniques. Real-time dashboards surface knowledge gaps by branch and role, so head office knows exactly where training is failing and why. Evaluation scorecards at individual, branch, and network level create accountability — turning learning into measurable operational performance across all 22 locations.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Premium Lens Upsell Failure
Optical dispensers who can't confidently articulate the difference between Crizal Easy, Crizal Prevencia, Transitions, EyeZen, and progressive lenses default to the cheapest option — and patients accept it. In a value-market practice, this is where margin lives or dies.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.5%
Annual LeakageR1.6m–R2.7m
Recovery @10%R162k–R270k
Effects
Patients leave with entry-level lenses when premium coatings or progressive options were clinically appropriate. Average basket value is suppressed branch-wide, and the gap compounds across 22 locations and thousands of dispensing encounters annually.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers role-specific microlearning on Essilor's full product range — matched to patient profile scenarios dispensers encounter daily. Dashboards show lens upsell rates by branch, flagging underperformers in real time so managers can intervene before the month's revenue is lost.
Leakage 02
Patient Recall & Retention Failure
Annual recall is the heartbeat of a sustainable optometry practice. When branch staff don't know how to execute a recall process, manage patient communication, or track lapsed appointments, thousands of warm patients quietly drift to competitors.
Loss Ratio15%–25%
Annual LeakageR1.8m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R180k–R300k
Effects
Lapsed patients represent lost eye test revenue and foregone dispensing income. The lifetime value of a recurring Optique patient is significant — losing even 10,000 annual recall patients means forgoing R12m in potential revenue across the network.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy trains and scorecard-tests branch managers on recall protocols and patient communication best practice. Management dashboards surface recall conversion rates by branch, identifying which locations are underperforming and enabling targeted intervention.
Leakage 03
Contact Lens Category Underpenetration
Contact lenses are a recurring-revenue category with strong lifetime value — but only when dispensers confidently identify fitting candidates, present trial lens options, and cross-sell from glasses to contacts. Insufficient product knowledge keeps this category chronically underpenetrated.
Loss Ratio20%–30%
Annual LeakageR2.0m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R200k–R300k
Effects
A glasses patient who also takes up contact lenses is worth materially more annually — and far more likely to return. Missed contact lens conversations at dispensing are missed subscription-style revenue that is difficult to recapture once the habit is formed elsewhere.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds contact lens candidate identification and CooperVision product knowledge into dispenser microlearning paths. Scorecards track cross-sell rates by branch, surfacing which locations are missing the contact lens opportunity at every dispensing encounter.
Leakage 04
HPCSA Compliance Exposure
Optique's R99 eye test model and its approach to prescription documentation operate under HPCSA Ethical Rules — and the rules are nuanced. Optometrists and branch staff who aren't current on HPCSA conduct requirements expose the business to formal complaints, sanctions, and branch-level reputational damage.
Risk Base22 branches
Annual LeakageR800k–R1.5m
Recovery @10%R80k–R150k
Effects
A single HPCSA complaint triggers investigation, legal cost, and negative online sentiment — all disproportionate to the volume of practice innovation Optique is driving. Across 22 branches with mixed staff tenure, compliance knowledge is uneven and under-tested.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers mandatory compliance microlearning on HPCSA conduct rules, prescription documentation obligations, and patient rights — with completion tracked at individual and branch level. Evaluation scorecards confirm comprehension, creating an auditable compliance record across the entire network.
Leakage 05
Frame Consultation Conversion Drop-off
A patient who walks in for a R99 eye test and walks out without glasses is a R1,800 average basket the practice will never recover. When frame consultants lack structured selling knowledge — needs identification, payment option fluency, lifestyle lens matching — conversion rates suffer quietly across every branch.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR1.1m–R2.2m
Recovery @10%R108k–R216k
Effects
Post-test conversion is the single highest-leverage moment in the Optique patient journey. Sub-optimal frame consultation means the R99 eye test investment generates no dispensing return — and the patient takes their prescription elsewhere.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces frame consultation skills — needs qualification, lifestyle lens matching, payment plan explanation, objection handling — through scenario-based microlearning specific to the Optique patient profile. Branch conversion rates become a tracked, manageable metric.
