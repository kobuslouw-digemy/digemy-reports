Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Premium Grade Conversion Gap
In regulated SA fuel pricing, per-litre margins are thin — which makes grade mix one of the few levers a forecourt operator can actually move. Attendants who cannot articulate why a vehicle benefits from 95 octane or Quartech diesel never make the ask, and the customer leaves on the grade they arrived with.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR75m–R150m
Recovery @10%R7.5m–R15m
Effects
Grade mix stays at the low end across tens of millions of annual transactions, suppressing per-litre margin across the network. Without a practised upgrade conversation, attendants default to filling whatever the customer requests — the Quartech diesel premium positioning never reaches the customer who could benefit from it.
The Digemy Difference
Spaced micromodules on grade specifications, vehicle suitability, natural upgrade triggers, and two-sentence objection responses ensure attendants have the language and the confidence to make the ask — every shift, not just after the last training day. Site manager dashboards track grade mix improvement by site week-on-week.
Leakage 02
Customer Retention Loss (Service Quality Decay)
In a market where competitor fuel stations are often metres apart, customer site loyalty is thin and entirely service-driven. Attendants who skip the vehicle check offer, fail to greet the customer, or rush the interaction actively push repeat business toward the nearest competitor — one poor experience at a time.
Loss Ratio0.4%–0.8%
Annual LeakageR60m–R120m
Recovery @10%R6m–R12m
Effects
Even a 0.4% annual loss of regular customers across 850 sites compounds into structural site-level share loss — customers who leave rarely return. Poor service creates negative word-of-mouth in community-linked retail environments where personal recommendation drives site preference.
The Digemy Difference
Service standard reinforcement through short scenario-based modules, embedded in shift routines, keeps customer engagement habits sharp without pulling attendants off the floor for lengthy briefings. Knowledge decay — which sets in within weeks of induction — is countered by continuous low-effort reinforcement that sites managers can track by individual.
Leakage 03
Loyalty Programme Under-activation
Astron Energy’s loyalty programme is a strategic retention asset — but its value is only realised when forecourt teams consistently enrol, scan, and activate customers at the pump. An attendant who doesn’t offer the card, explain the benefit, or scan at point of sale leaves the programme’s return on the forecourt every single shift.
Loss Ratio0.3%–0.6%
Annual LeakageR45m–R90m
Recovery @10%R4.5m–R9m
Effects
Under-enrolled customers visit less frequently, spend less per visit on average, and defect to competitor sites far more easily. The programme infrastructure cost is incurred regardless — the return is lost to low activation rates that compound with every shift change and new hire.
The Digemy Difference
Loyalty enrolment scripts, benefit articulation, and scan-at-pump protocols are delivered as a standing reinforcement track — ensuring every new attendant and every returning hire maintains activation discipline without depending on a manager to remind them before each shift.
Leakage 04
Convenience Store Cross-sell Failure
The pump-to-store journey is one of fuel retail’s most proven margin levers — customers who enter the convenience store after fuelling spend significantly more than walk-in customers. Attendants who don’t prompt the transition, mention the daily special, or link fuelling to a store offer leave this uplift unrealised on every transaction.
Loss Ratio3%–6%
Annual LeakageR45m–R90m
Recovery @10%R4.5m–R9m
Effects
Convenience store revenue consistently under-performs relative to forecourt traffic volumes. Promotional investment in daily specials and meal deals goes largely unrecouped because the message never reaches the customer at the pump — the highest-traffic moment in the site’s day.
The Digemy Difference
Weekly promotion briefings delivered to attendants via mobile — covering current in-store specials, meal deals, and bundle offers — ensure the pump interaction consistently drives store traffic. Site managers confirm promotion knowledge before the shift starts, not after it’s over.
Leakage 05
Lubricants & Car Care Upsell Failure
The vehicle check is one of the forecourt’s most underleveraged customer moments — oil level, screen wash, wiper condition, and tyre pressure are all observable triggers for a product recommendation. Attendants who skip the check or lack the knowledge to recommend Havoline or car care products miss the upsell entirely and lose the sale to a workshop or supermarket down the road.
Loss Ratio5%–10%
Annual LeakageR25m–R50m
Recovery @10%R2.5m–R5m
Effects
Lubricant and accessories revenue stays below its potential at site level as customers make ad hoc purchases elsewhere. Skipped vehicle checks also create a service liability exposure — customers who later report undisclosed issues can reflect poorly on the site and on the Astron Energy brand.
The Digemy Difference
Short vehicle check protocol modules with embedded product recommendation scripts ensure attendants know exactly what to look for, which Havoline product to suggest for a given top-up need, and how to frame the recommendation in under 20 seconds. Consistent behaviour across shifts drives compounding upsell revenue.
