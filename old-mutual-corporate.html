EB Consultants and claims staff who misapply regulations create an estimated 0.5%–1.0% compliance exposure on Old Mutual Corporate’s group risk premium base.

Annual LeakageR40m–R80m
Recovery @10%R4.0m–R8.0m
Effects
FSCA enforcement actions, OMBUD referrals and civil claims don’t just cost money — they threaten FSP license standing and reputational capital with the corporate employer market. One compliance failure can invite sector-wide scrutiny.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s regulatory update engine pushes FAIS, FSCA and two-pot compliance content to every frontline role the moment regulation changes, with verified assessments ensuring retention — not just acknowledgement.

OM Corporate processed 170,000 two-pot claims in 2024; inadequately counselled withdrawals erode AUM and drive employer dissatisfaction at renewal.
