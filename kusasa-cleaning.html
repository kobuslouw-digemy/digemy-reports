Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Site Quality & SLA Consistency Gaps
Multi-site cleaning quality depends entirely on whether each on-site team executes the client's exact SLA checklist, every shift, without a supervisor present to catch drift. Where knowledge of a specific client's standards fades between induction and daily practice, checklist items get skipped and complaints follow.
Loss Ratio1.2%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR1.14m–R1.9m
Recovery @10%R114k–R190k
Effects
Recurring client complaints erode the account relationship long before a formal contract review, and unbilled rework visits absorb labour hours. Persistent SLA drift is one of the most common root causes of non-renewal in contract cleaning.
The Digemy Difference
Spaced-repetition refreshers keep each site team's exact SLA checklist top-of-mind between supervisor visits, not just at induction. Managers get visibility into which sites are drifting before the client notices.
Leakage 02
H&S & Chemical Handling Compliance Gaps
Contract cleaning teams handle industrial-strength chemicals and work under the OHS Act and COID framework daily, often with safety training delivered once at onboarding and rarely reinforced. When protocol knowledge decays, incidents, near-misses and compliance exposure follow.
Loss Ratio0.8%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR760k–R1.425m
Recovery @10%R76k–R142.5k
Effects
Safety incidents trigger COID claims, site downtime and client-side audits that can pause or terminate a contract. Chemical misuse also damages client property, creating liability the company absorbs directly.
The Digemy Difference
Ongoing micro-refreshers on chemical handling, PPE and incident protocol keep safety knowledge current across a mobile, multi-site workforce head office can't supervise every shift.
Leakage 03
Specialised Services Technical Errors
Pest control, high-access window cleaning and strip/seal floor work are higher-margin, higher-skill services where a technical error is costly to fix and can damage trust in the wider contract. These roles depend on certification-level knowledge that standard induction alone doesn't sustain over time.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR237.5k–R475k
Recovery @10%R23.75k–R47.5k
Effects
A single high-access or pest-control error can mean a redo visit, a damage claim, or — in the worst case — a safety incident that puts the entire specialised contract at risk.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific spaced learning keeps technical protocols and certification knowledge sharp for smaller, specialised technician teams where one knowledge gap has an outsized cost.
Leakage 04
New Contract Mobilisation & Onboarding Shortfall
Every new site win — and every integration such as the 2025 Metro Sanitary Solutions acquisition — requires rapidly onboarding teams to a new client's specific standards and layout. Slow, inconsistent onboarding is a leading driver of early-contract dissatisfaction, right when the relationship is most fragile.
Loss Ratio20%–30%
Annual LeakageR2.28m–R3.42m
Recovery @10%R228k–R342k
Effects
A shaky first 90 days on a new contract sets the tone for the entire relationship and is a leading cause of early churn, undermining the sales effort that won the contract.
The Digemy Difference
A structured onboarding learning pathway gets new and transferred teams to full site-specific competence faster, protecting the critical early-contract period on every mobilisation.
Leakage 05
Consumables & Chemical Dosing Wastage
Over- or under-dosing cleaning chemicals and mishandling equipment quietly inflates the cost of every contract, without ever showing up as a single visible failure. Left unchecked, this knowledge gap erodes margin on every site, every month.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR142.5k–R190k
Recovery @10%R14.25k–R19k
Effects
Chemical wastage and equipment misuse raise the direct cost of service delivery on every contract, compressing margin that's already thin in a competitive, labour-intensive industry.
The Digemy Difference
Reinforced dosing and equipment-care protocols reduce wastage at the point of use, protecting margin without adding a single extra supervisory visit.
