Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Plant Operator Competency & Unplanned Downtime
With 150+ machines deployed across the Western Cape, operators who don't recognise early warning signs, follow maintenance schedules, or operate within correct parameters turn routine jobs into costly breakdowns. Every unplanned machine day off-hire is lost revenue and client disruption.
Loss Ratio1.5%–3%
Annual LeakageR7.5m–R15m
Recovery @10%R750k–R1.5m
Effects
Unplanned breakdowns reduce fleet utilisation, trigger emergency repairs, and displace project timelines. Clients experiencing repeat downtime incidents reduce contract scope or move to competitors.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces pre-start inspection checklists, machine-specific operating limits, and maintenance trigger recognition through daily micro-learning — keeping every operator current and audit-ready.
Leakage 02
OHS & Lifting Equipment Non-Compliance
Crane operations and rigging are among the highest-risk activities on any construction site. Gaps in OHS Act knowledge, DMR lifting machine regulations, and SANS lifting standards expose QuickSILVA to incidents, project shutdowns, CIDB penalty points, and litigation that dwarf the cost of prevention.
Loss RatioRisk-based
Annual LeakageR6m–R14m
Recovery @10%R600k–R1.4m
Effects
A single serious lifting incident triggers DoL investigations, mandatory project stoppages, and potential CIDB grading downgrade — costing weeks of lost revenue and long-term reputational damage with government and developer clients.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps crane operators, riggers, and supervisors continuously current on OHS Act requirements, DMR regulations, and site-specific lifting plans through role-targeted reinforcement and real-time compliance dashboards.
Leakage 03
Structural Steel Rework & Fabrication Defects
Steel fabrication and erection tolerances are tight — a welder operating on outdated procedure knowledge or a fitter misreading structural specifications creates rework that burns material, delays handover, and erodes project margin. On large turnkey projects, even a 1.5% rework rate on a R300m construction base is significant.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.5%
Annual LeakageR4.5m–R7.5m
Recovery @10%R450k–R750k
Effects
Fabrication rework delays project completion, triggers penalty clauses on fixed-price contracts, and inflates material costs. Repeat quality issues undermine QuickSILVA's reputation for precision steel erection built over 38 years.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds welding procedure specifications, tolerance checklists, and quality sign-off protocols into daily reinforcement for fitters, welders, and erectors — surfacing gaps by team and site before they reach the customer.
Leakage 04
Heavy Haulage Compliance & Permit Failures
Moving loads from 4T to 55T across South African road networks demands precise knowledge of abnormal load permits, axle load regulations, and route clearance requirements. Drivers and logistics coordinators without current knowledge face fines, load detentions, and client-facing delays that compound across nationwide contracts.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR2m–R4m
Recovery @10%R200k–R400k
Effects
Permit violations trigger SANRAL and traffic authority fines, forced off-loading, and project delays — especially costly on shutdown contracts where every hour counts. Repeated incidents jeopardise operating licences.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces abnormal load regulations, permit requirements, and route compliance protocols for QuickSILVATranz drivers and coordinators — ensuring every move is legally defensible before wheels roll.
Leakage 05
Supervisor & Site Manager Scope Knowledge Gaps
Site supervisors and foremen are the commercial nerve of every QuickSILVA project — translating client requirements into operational reality. When they lack current knowledge of service scope, SLA terms, or variation procedures, the result is underquoted scope creep, unrecovered extras, and client disputes that erode both margin and relationships.

The Digemy Difference
Digemy baselines and continuously reinforces scope management, SLA terms, variation procedures, and client communication protocols for supervisors — with management dashboards that identify which sites carry the highest knowledge risk.
