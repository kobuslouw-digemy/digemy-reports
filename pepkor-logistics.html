Why Digemy

Pepkor Logistics moves stock through 19 distribution centres to more than 5,000 stores a week, at a speed and scale where a small drop in frontline consistency compounds fast. Pick errors, dock delays and route inefficiency rarely show up as a single line item — they surface as returns, stockouts and rising cost-to-serve. Digemy closes the knowledge gaps at the point where they actually happen: on the pick line, at the dock, and behind the wheel.

“In a network moving half a billion units a year, a knowledge gap isn’t a training issue — it’s a margin issue.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy converts DC and fleet SOPs into short, role-specific microlearning delivered where frontline teams already are — on the floor, on shift, in the cab. Faster time-to-competency for seasonal intake, fewer repeat errors, and measurable accuracy gains translate directly into recovered rand, not just improved training scores.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Pick & Dispatch Accuracy Errors
Wrong-SKU picks and incomplete scan-verification at peak throughput drive returns, re-delivery runs and store-level stockouts. The cost multiplies when an error reaches a store 200km from the DC.
Loss Ratio0.3%–0.6%
Annual LeakageR207m–R414m
Recovery @10%R21m–R41m
Effects
Mis-picks trigger costly reverse-logistics runs and erode store trust in DC replenishment. New and seasonal pickers account for a disproportionate share of errors in their first weeks.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds scan-verification microlearning directly at the pick station and compresses time-to-competency for seasonal intake, so accuracy holds even as volume peaks.
Leakage 02
Stock Damage & Shrinkage in DC Handling
Inconsistent handling technique on forklifts, reach-trucks and conveyor interfaces damages stock before it ever reaches a store. Unrecorded shrinkage further distorts inventory accuracy.
Loss Ratio0.2%–0.5%
Annual LeakageR138m–R345m
Recovery @10%R14m–R35m
Effects
Damaged stock is written off or discounted, and shrinkage gaps undermine the accuracy of replenishment planning across the CGM store base.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific handling and equipment modules, refreshed on a cycle tied to actual damage data, target the exact behaviours driving loss on each DC floor.
Leakage 03
Dock & Turnaround Delays
Inconsistent loading sequencing and documentation at the dock slow truck turnaround, pushing back delivery windows to stores that depend on tight weekly schedules.
Loss Ratio0.15%–0.4%
Annual LeakageR103m–R276m
Recovery @10%R10m–R28m
Effects
Slow turnaround ripples through the whole route plan, delaying downstream store deliveries and increasing overtime and standing-truck costs.
The Digemy Difference
Standardised, checklist-driven dock training shortens loading sequences and reduces documentation rework, keeping trucks moving on schedule.
Leakage 04
Fleet & Route Inefficiency
Despite route-optimisation tools already in place, inconsistent driver behaviour and adherence still leave fuel and time savings on the table across third-party fleet partners.
Loss Ratio0.2%–0.45%
Annual LeakageR138m–R311m
Recovery @10%R14m–R31m
Effects
Excess fuel burn, idling and empty running erode the savings the route-optimisation system was designed to capture, adding avoidable cost per delivery.
The Digemy Difference
Driver-behaviour coaching content tied to telematics data reinforces the optimised routes with the habits needed to actually realise the planned savings.
Leakage 05
PAXI & Parcel Service Failures
As the largest counter-to-counter parcel service in South Africa, PAXI's reputation rests on frontline counter accuracy; mis-sorts and delays at high-volume counters translate directly into customer complaints.
Loss Ratio0.1%–0.3%
Annual LeakageR69m–R207m
Recovery @10%R7m–R21m
Effects
Lost or mis-sorted parcels damage customer trust in a service built on convenience and cost, and drive avoidable re-sends and counter-side escalations.
The Digemy Difference
Counter-staff microlearning on sort-verification and escalation protocol closes the gap fastest at the highest-volume, highest-risk counters.
