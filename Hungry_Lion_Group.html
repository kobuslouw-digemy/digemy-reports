Why Digemy

Hungry Lion operates 550+ outlets across 20+ countries, relying on a frontline workforce that must execute consistently on upsell protocols, portioning standards, food safety requirements, and new product launches — without a reliable mechanism to verify that knowledge has actually landed. Turnover is high, onboarding timelines are stretched across markets with different languages and franchise structures, yet every outlet is benchmarked against the same operational and financial standards. Digemy closes that gap by baseline-mapping knowledge by role, reinforcing it through spaced microlearning, and surfacing real-time performance gaps by outlet, market, and team before they become revenue leakage.

“In QSR, the counter is your highest-leverage revenue moment — and most chains are training for speed, not for selling.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy baseline-maps knowledge across every frontline role — from counter crew to franchise operators — and delivers spaced reinforcement to lock in upsell scripts, portioning standards, and SOP adherence across all markets. Management dashboards surface knowledge gaps by outlet and country in real time, while evaluation scorecards hold individual crew, shift supervisors, and regional managers accountable to the same standard. The result: every Hungry Lion outlet executing at full potential, every day, regardless of geography.
