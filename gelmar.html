Why Digemy

Gelmar’s frontline sales staff are the single most important variable between a customer’s renovation vision and the right product leaving the shelf — yet with 50 stores, 300+ handle styles, and a fast-expanding house-brand range, knowledge consistency across branches is structurally impossible without a reinforcement platform. When an internal sales assistant can’t confidently guide a contractor or DIYer from question to solution, the sale walks. Digemy turns product knowledge into a measurable, branch-level competitive advantage — reinforced daily, tracked in real time, and applied where it counts.

“In a store where every handle has a twin and every customer has a deadline, the staff who know the range sell the range.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy maps and baselines product knowledge across every Gelmar store role — from internal sales assistants to merchandise controllers. Spaced reinforcement keeps house-brand advocacy, new-range specifications, and upsell techniques sharp across all 50 branches, while real-time dashboards surface knowledge gaps by store and role. Individual- and site-level scorecards give branch managers and Gelmar leadership the accountability tools to drive consistent conversion, reduce returns, and grow basket size with confidence.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
House Brand & New Range Advocacy Gap
Gelmar’s growing house-brand range offers the highest margin per unit — yet staff who don’t know the product can’t recommend it. Without structured reinforcement, new range launches land on the shelf but not in the sales conversation, leaving the highest-value revenue stream chronically underleveraged.
Loss Ratio20%–30%
Annual LeakageR13.5m–R20.2m
Recovery @10%R1.4m–R2.0m
Effects
Customers leave with branded competitor products or delay purchase entirely. House-brand margin potential goes unrealised and new launches fail to gain floor-level traction despite availability.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys product-specific reinforcement modules at new range launch, with scorecards that measure advocacy at store and individual level — ensuring every sales assistant can articulate the house-brand value proposition on day one of availability.
Leakage 02
Sales Conversion Failure
Gelmar is a destination store — customers arrive with a project in mind but leave without converting when staff can’t bridge the gap between the product and the project. In a high-SKU specialty environment, that advisory gap is the difference between a sale and a lost basket.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR4.5m–R6.8m
Recovery @10%R0.5m–R0.7m
Effects
Walk-in traffic converts at below-potential rates. Customers default to online or competitor purchases when in-store guidance is uncertain or inconsistent across branches.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s knowledge-retention modules ensure every internal sales assistant is fluent in product application, technical specs, and customer use-case scenarios — turning advisory capability into a branch-level conversion driver.
Leakage 03
Cross-sell & Basket Underperformance
A customer buying drawer runners needs hinges, handles, and mounting hardware — but only gets them if the staff member knows to suggest them. With a product range spanning thousands of SKUs across kitchen, bathroom, and furniture fittings, cross-sell knowledge is the engine of basket growth.
Loss Ratio0.8%–1.2%
Annual LeakageR3.6m–R5.4m
Recovery @10%R0.4m–R0.5m
Effects
Average transaction values remain flat. Complementary products sit unsold in adjacent aisles while customers make multiple trips or source them elsewhere — shrinking share-of-wallet per visit.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds cross-category product linkages into reinforcement modules, creating an instinctive upsell habit in frontline staff — with dashboards that track basket-size improvements by store and team.
Leakage 04
Returns & Wrong-Product Advisory
Incorrect product recommendations are the hidden cost multiplier in hardware retail: the return, the restock, the exchange labour, the shipping cost for online orders, and the customer who doesn’t come back. In a product category defined by exact specifications and compatibility, advisory errors are expensive.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR2.3m–R4.5m
Recovery @10%R0.2m–R0.5m
Effects
Return rates inflate operational cost and erode margin. Customers who receive wrong advice experience project delays, lose trust in the brand, and default to self-research rather than in-store consultation on future visits.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces technical specifications, compatibility requirements, and application guidance at the role level — reducing wrong-product advice at source and turning knowledge confidence into a measurable returns-reduction metric.
Leakage 05
Merchandise & Stock Accuracy Losses
Gelmar’s high-SKU environment — with hundreds of handle styles, finishes, and size variants — creates significant exposure to stock-take errors, mislabelling, and incorrect receiving. These losses compound silently across 50 branches, representing both direct shrinkage and invisible margin erosion.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.4m–R2.7m
Recovery @10%R0.1m–R0.3m
Effects
Inventory inaccuracies result in phantom stockouts, over-ordering, and write-offs. Planogram non-compliance means high-margin products are invisible to customers, further suppressing conversion.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy trains and reinforces receiving protocols, planogram standards, and stock-take procedures by role — with branch-level scorecards that make merchandise compliance a tracked, accountable metric rather than a periodic audit event.
