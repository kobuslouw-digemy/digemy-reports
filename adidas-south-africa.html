Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
New Product Launch Adoption Shortfall
Every major drop — from Boost cushioning launches to the Bafana Bafana and Banyana Banyana kits — depends on floor staff who can tell the product story from day one. When training lags the launch calendar, hero product windows close before staff catch up.
Loss Ratio20%–40%
Annual LeakageR78m–R156m
Recovery @10%R7.8m–R15.6m
Effects
Launch-week footfall converts at a discount because staff default to generic pitches instead of the specific technology story. Hero styles get marked down faster than they should, eroding margin on the products designed to carry it.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers launch-ready refreshers to every store before doors open, tied to the season's drop calendar. Store teams walk the floor knowing the product story cold, not catching up once the buzz has faded.
Leakage 02
Technical Product Knowledge Gaps at POS
Boost, Lightstrike, Primeknit — Adidas sells on technology, and customers expect staff to explain it. When associates can't answer a fit or performance question with confidence, baskets shrink and customers go compare online instead.
Loss Ratio0.5%–2%
Annual LeakageR13m–R52m
Recovery @10%R1.3m–R5.2m
Effects
Lower average transaction value as add-on and upsell conversations don't happen. Customers who don't get a confident answer in-store frequently complete the purchase decision elsewhere.
The Digemy Difference
Short, repeated refreshers keep technology stories and current-season knowledge sharp across every store, not just flagship locations. New starters reach floor-ready product fluency in days, not weeks.
Leakage 03
Loss Prevention & Stock Accuracy Gaps
Stockroom procedure lapses — from receiving checks to daily counts — are one of the quietest sources of shrinkage in apparel retail. Inconsistent adherence across stores compounds unnoticed over a full trading year.
Loss Ratio0.5%–2%
Annual LeakageR13m–R52m
Recovery @10%R1.3m–R5.2m
Effects
Stock discrepancies distort replenishment decisions and tie up capital in phantom inventory. Shrinkage variance between stores points to procedure gaps, not just opportunity.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces receiving, counting, and loss-prevention procedure until it becomes habit, with manager visibility into which stores need a refresher. Consistency replaces store-by-store variation.
Leakage 04
Adiclub Enrollment & Data Capture Gaps
Every customer who leaves without an Adiclub sign-up is a repeat-purchase relationship Adidas already paid to acquire, lost at the till. Enrollment habits vary widely by associate and by store.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR13m–R39m
Recovery @10%R1.3m–R3.9m
Effects
Lower repeat-visit rates and weaker first-party data for future campaigns. Loyalty-driven revenue that should compound instead resets with every transaction.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds the enrollment ask into everyday coaching, not a once-off onboarding module. Store leaders see enrollment rates by team member and close the gap directly.
Leakage 05
Returns & Exchange Policy Inconsistency
Inconsistent handling of returns and exchanges across stores creates both margin leakage and customer friction. Policy exceptions handled differently store to store erode trust in the in-store experience.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR13m–R39m
Recovery @10%R1.3m–R3.9m
Effects
Avoidable refunds and goodwill exchanges outside policy quietly erode margin. Inconsistent experiences push repeat customers toward Adidas' own e-commerce or competitors.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps policy and edge-case guidance current and consistently applied across every store. Associates get quick, confident answers in the moment, not guesswork.
