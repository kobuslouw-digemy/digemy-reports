Why Digemy
BON Hotels has built one of South Africa's most ambitious hotel management platforms — 40+ properties, a growing management contract pipeline, and a brand built on the promise of consistent, warm African hospitality. The challenge is one every multi-site hospitality operator knows but rarely solves: when your product is delivered by thousands of frontline staff across dozens of properties, every inconsistency in service behaviour, product knowledge or complaint handling becomes a leakage event. A server who can't describe the menu, a front desk agent who doesn't offer an upgrade, a housekeeper who misses an amenity — these aren't one-off incidents. They're compounding daily across every property in the portfolio, suppressing RevPAR, eroding review scores and converting potential repeat guests into one-time visitors.

Digemy exists to solve exactly this problem. Our microlearning platform doesn't replace BON's operational leadership — it amplifies it. By converting your brand standards, SOPs and service frameworks into bite-sized, spaced-repetition micro-modules deployed directly to frontline staff on their phones, Digemy builds the knowledge retention and behavioural consistency that traditional face-to-face training cannot sustain at scale. The result is a portfolio-wide uplift in service quality, commercial behaviour and guest experience — measurable at the property level, actionable by management, and sustained far beyond the initial training event.

“In a hotel, your brand isn’t the lobby or the linen — it’s the 22-year-old at the front desk who decides in three seconds whether a guest becomes a loyal repeat visitor or a one-star review.”

Leakage 03
Housekeeping Standard & Guest Satisfaction Failure
Housekeeping is the invisible engine of a hotel's guest satisfaction score. When rooms are turned to inconsistent standards — missed amenities, incorrect linen, slow turnaround or poor presentation — guests leave negative reviews that suppress future occupancy and ADR (average daily rate). One bad room can override an otherwise excellent stay.

The Digemy Difference
Embeds SOPs for room checklist discipline, amenity placement standards, linen protocols and turnaround timing — building the daily habits that keep review scores high and repeat guests confident in the product.
Leakage 04
Complaint Handling & Service Recovery Failure
In hospitality, a well-handled complaint creates a more loyal guest than an issue-free stay. When frontline staff don't know how to acknowledge, empathise, resolve and follow up on complaints — or escalate without first attempting resolution — the property loses the guest permanently and gains a public negative review.

Conference and events business is high-margin and high-stakes. Digemy Skills Matrix — BON Hotels Portfolio

Digemy's approach for BON Hotels begins by mapping every frontline role across the portfolio to its highest-impact knowledge gap. For F&B teams, this means menu fluency, pairing confidence and upsell behaviour — built through weekly micro-modules that sync with seasonal menu changes and are pushed directly to staff phones before each shift. For front desk agents, the skills matrix anchors on three commercial moments: the upgrade conversation at check-in, the loyalty enrolment offer, and the first-contact complaint resolution — each reinforced through scenario-based micro-learning that builds habit through repetition, not once-off classroom sessions.

For housekeeping and events teams, Digemy builds SOP compliance through visual checklist micro-modules and pre-shift briefing tools that ensure no step is missed regardless of staff experience level. The result across the BON Hotels portfolio is a consistent, measurable uplift in the behaviours that drive RevPAR, guest satisfaction scores, repeat bookings and F&B margin — delivered at the speed of a managed hotel network growing its brand one property at a time.
