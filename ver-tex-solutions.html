Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Agent Negotiation & PTP Conversion Gap
Across 1,000 commission-model agents handling high-volume unsecured debt, negotiation skill variance is the single largest driver of revenue performance. Without continuous reinforcement, agents default to passive scripts — missing the affordability probing, objection-handling, and commitment-anchoring techniques that separate a secured PTP from a hung-up call.
Loss Ratio6%–10% of commission
Annual LeakageR7.0m–R12.0m
Recovery @10%R700k–R1.2m
Effects
Lower PTP conversion rates directly reduce commission earned per agent; unresolved accounts roll to expensive legal stages, cutting client margins. Industry data shows mean PTP rates at 29%, with best-in-class operations exceeding 50% — the gap is almost entirely a training and reinforcement problem.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s spaced-reinforcement platform delivers bite-sized negotiation modules — objection handling, affordability probing, commitment anchoring — on a schedule that prevents knowledge decay and keeps technique consistent across every agent, every shift.
Leakage 02
Regulatory Compliance Breach (NCA / POPIA / Debt Collectors Act)
Ver-tex operates in one of South Africa’s most regulated sectors — the National Credit Act, POPIA data obligations, and the Debt Collectors Act govern every agent interaction. A single non-compliant call can trigger a Council for Debt Collectors inquiry, client-directed penalty clauses, or a formal POPIA complaint to the Information Regulator.
Loss Ratio1%–2% of commission
Annual LeakageR1.2m–R2.4m
Recovery @10%R120k–R240k
Effects
Council registration risk, client contract loss and POPIA Information Regulator fines of up to R10m create existential exposure beyond the direct revenue figure. Compliance drift is invisible until it becomes a crisis — by which point the cost is orders of magnitude higher than prevention.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys compliance modules as auditable, completion-tracked micro-bursts — giving management evidence of regulatory readiness and giving agents a continuous, non-disruptive reminder of exactly what they can and cannot say on each call type.
Leakage 03
Agent Attrition & New-Hire Ramp Gap
South African call centres run annual attrition well above 30%; debt collection environments, with their performance pressure and compliance demands, trend considerably higher. At 1,000 agents, this means 350–500 exits per year — each position replaced by someone spending three to five months at sub-optimal productivity while the next agent learns the ropes from scratch.
Loss Ratio35%–50% annual churn
Annual LeakageR8.0m–R14.0m
Recovery @10%R800k–R1.4m
Effects
Escalating recruitment, onboarding and training costs combine with a prolonged productivity gap that directly suppresses commission revenue. The revolving door also dilutes team culture and erodes supervisor bandwidth — supervisors spend time babysitting new hires instead of coaching performers.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s proof point is a 70% reduction in ramp time for FLM Academy’s frontline workforce — at Ver-tex scale, this directly shortens the productivity gap for hundreds of annual new hires and begins generating commission-contributing output weeks sooner.
Leakage 04
Right-Party Contact & Data Quality Gap
Industry benchmarks place average Right-Party Contact (RPC) rates at around 26%, meaning roughly three in four outbound calls never reach the person who can actually resolve the debt. Poor trace pack quality, stale contact data, and inconsistent multi-channel strategy drive this figure lower — consuming agent capacity that should be generating commission.
Loss Ratio15%–25% capacity wasted
Annual LeakageR5.0m–R8.0m
Recovery @10%R500k–R800k
Effects
Wasted call minutes erode commission throughput and drive agent frustration — itself a contributor to attrition. Clients who measure SLA recovery rates start questioning agency value when RPC weakness is opaque and unaddressed.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces trace accuracy standards, multi-channel contact sequencing, and data quality disciplines as operational micro-modules — translating training into higher RPC rates and more productive calling minutes across the floor.
Leakage 05
PTP Arrangement Default (Payment Promise Kept-Rate Gap)
Securing a promise to pay is only half the collection. Industry data shows mean PTP rates at 29%, with a significant portion of those arrangements defaulting before completion — often because agents set up commitments that are unaffordable or fail to use reinforcement language that makes the arrangement feel binding. Accounts that re-enter the queue do so in a deteriorated, harder-to-collect state.
Loss Ratio8%–15% of secured PTPs
Annual LeakageR5.0m–R9.0m
Recovery @10%R500k–R900k
Effects
Arrangement defaults push accounts toward litigation — the most expensive recovery channel. Client recovery targets are missed; agent commission on lapsed accounts is forfeited; account queue ages and collectability declines with every cycle.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy trains agents on affordability-anchored arrangement structuring, commitment language and post-PTP follow-through technique — reducing lapse rates and keeping hard-won promises on track to convert into collected commission.
