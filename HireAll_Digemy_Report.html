Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Hire Desk Upsell & Cross-Sell Failures
Desk consultants who don’t know the full product range — which accessories pair with which tools, which consumables are required, when a protection plan makes sense — default to completing the basic transaction rather than building a complete solution for the customer. At 15,000 transactions per year, even a modest uplift in upsell conversion materially compounds across the portfolio.
Missed Upsell Rate30%–40%
Annual LeakageR675k–R1.8m
Recovery @10%R68k–R180k
Effects
Transaction values stay at the floor rather than the ceiling, compressing margin per hire. Customers who needed accessories leave without them — return to get them from a competitor — and sometimes don’t come back at all. Protection plan attachment rates stay chronically low, increasing unrecovered damage exposure.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces product pairing knowledge, consultative questioning technique, and protection plan presentation through spaced repetition modules — so consultants don’t just know the catalogue, they know how to use it to build a higher-value hire for every customer.
Leakage 02
Wrong Tool Recommendations & Revenue-Destroying Returns
When a consultant recommends the wrong equipment for a customer’s job — underpowered, incompatible, or simply the wrong category — the hire ends early, generates a return trip, and often requires a re-hire of the correct item. Each incident burns margin, consumes staff time, and hands the customer a story they’ll tell other contractors.
Wrong-Tool Hire Rate8%–12%
Annual LeakageR600k–R2.16m
Recovery @10%R60k–R216k
Effects
Return and re-hire admin consumes hire desk capacity that should be serving new customers. Equipment is returned early and sits idle while the replacement hire is processed, compressing asset utilisation. NPS scores drop with every wrong recommendation that the customer has to fix themselves.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds deep equipment knowledge through scenario-based application modules — consultants practice matching tool specifications to real job scenarios, reducing wrong-tool hires by keeping application knowledge sharp and constantly refreshed across the full range.
Leakage 03
Customer Churn from Poor Service Knowledge
Tool hire is a repeat-purchase business — contractors, builders, and tradespeople hire regularly and consolidate with one or two trusted suppliers. When the desk experience is uninformed, slow, or inconsistent, returning customers don’t complain; they quietly ring the competitor next time. In a market with multiple alternatives, CX quality is the stickiest retention lever HireAll controls.
Revenue at Risk (CX churn)1%–2%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R3.0m
Recovery @10%R150k–R300k
Effects
Lost returning customers represent the highest-margin revenue — no acquisition cost, established credit terms, predictable booking patterns. Each defection is largely invisible until the booking history is reviewed and the customer is already entrenched with a competitor.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy embeds consistent CX standards — greeting, needs assessment, professional recommendation, closing — as reinforced habits across all hire desk and customer service staff, so every interaction delivers the confidence and speed that converts a first-time hirer into a returning account.
Leakage 04
Unrecovered Equipment Damage
Hire desk consultants and drivers who haven’t been rigorously trained on damage inspection standards — what to document, how to document it, and how to have the damage conversation with a customer — routinely fail to recover legitimate repair costs. This erodes equipment value, inflates maintenance costs, and subsidises customers who return equipment in worse condition than they received it.
Unrecovered Damage Rate3%–5%
Annual LeakageR360k–R1.5m
Recovery @10%R36k–R150k
Effects
Fleet maintenance budgets absorb costs that should be charged to the hirer. Equipment availability falls as damaged assets sit awaiting repair rather than generating hire revenue. Staff avoid difficult damage conversations because they lack the training and process confidence to handle disputes professionally.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys damage inspection and recovery process modules with scenario-based practice, building the confidence and procedural consistency that ensures staff identify, document, and recover damage costs accurately — on every return, at every branch.

Field sales reps and desk staff who lack the knowledge to speak a contractor’s language, understand project timelines, and propose account terms lose these high-value relationships to competitors with more informed sales teams.
