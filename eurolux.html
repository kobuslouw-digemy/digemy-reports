Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Project & Commercial Specification Accuracy
Project lighting is Eurolux’s fastest-growing and highest-margin segment — but a misspecified luminaire (wrong IP rating, wrong CCT, wrong wattage) means a rejected installation, a costly credit note, and a contractor who calls a competitor next time. With 5,000+ SKUs and a steady pipeline of new commercial products, reps who can’t spec with precision lose projects before the quote is even submitted.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR5.4m–R7.2m
Recovery @10%R540k–R720k
Effects
Incorrect commercial specs generate costly returns, damage contractor relationships, and cede project opportunities to more technically capable competitors. Repeat misspecification accelerates account churn in a segment where relationship depth defines long-term revenue.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy builds product specification knowledge directly into daily microlearning — lumen performance, IP classifications, CCT by application, and SABS compliance — so reps arrive at every project conversation with the confidence to specify and win.

Trade Channel Pull-Through
Electrical wholesalers and retail/DIY partners stock dozens of competing lighting brands. Eurolux’s trade reps need to be the most product-knowledgeable person in the room — capable of ranging conversations, planogram recommendations, and real-time product substitutions. Knowledge gaps cede shelf space and channel mindshare to better-prepared competitors at the point of range review.
Loss Ratio1.0%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR4.8m–R7.2m
Recovery @10%R480k–R720k
Effects
Reps who can’t articulate differentiation across competing SKUs lose ranging battles, see own-brand substitutions take hold, and watch trade partners default to alternative suppliers. Every account where Eurolux is under-ranged is a structural revenue gap compounding month on month.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps trade reps current on full range positioning, competitive differentiation, and promotional strategies — so every trade visit builds range depth and drives pull-through rather than eroding it.

Sales staff who aren’t fluent in new product positioning default to the familiar ranges they already know, leaving new-product revenue unrealised across the entire distribution network from day one of launch.

Annual LeakageR20m–R28m

Effects
When frontline teams can’t confidently present new ranges, launch momentum dissipates within weeks — and premium-margin products sit in the warehouse while lower-margin commodity SKUs are sold instead. NPD investment is wasted if the team can’t activate it.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys structured new-product launch learning tracks, verified by knowledge checks, so every rep can present, position, and sell new ranges from day one of launch — not week six.
Leakage 04
SKU & Order Accuracy Costs

Each error generates a credit note, a return logistics cost, a re-delivery, and a frustrated customer. Multiplied across tens of thousands of order lines annually, SKU knowledge gaps become a meaningful operational and margin cost.

Annual LeakageR6.0m–R9.6m
Recovery @10%R600k–R960k
Effects
Order errors erode gross margin through credit notes and logistics costs while simultaneously damaging customer trust and increasing admin burden across the supply chain. High-frequency SKU confusion is both a profitability problem and a customer retention risk.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s bite-sized product category modules — targeted at internal sales teams and trade counter staff — build SKU recognition and order accuracy, reducing costly errors at every point of order entry.

When technical advice is inconsistent, claims handling is slow, or complaints escalate without resolution, trade partners shift their purchasing to better-serviced competitors. In a market where Eurolux competes on both product breadth and service reputation, knowledge-driven service failures are a direct and often invisible revenue risk.
