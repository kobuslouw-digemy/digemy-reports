Why Digemy

TooMuchWifi's promise — fast, affordable internet in South Africa's most underserved communities — is only as strong as the frontline knowledge that installs it, activates it, and keeps subscribers connected. From community technicians commissioning access points in townships to resellers fielding upgrade questions at spaza shops, execution quality lives entirely in what the frontline knows and retains.

Where the loss happens — an estimated R9.75m–R19.5m a year:

Subscriber Churn Upsell Failures Field Rework Agent Underperformance Launch Delays
“In township connectivity, the difference between a subscriber who stays and one who churns is sixty seconds of good troubleshooting — and those sixty seconds live entirely in your frontline's head.”

Digemy maps and baselines knowledge across every TooMuchWifi frontline role — Community Network Technician, Community Sales Agent, Customer Support Agent and Field Operations Engineer.
