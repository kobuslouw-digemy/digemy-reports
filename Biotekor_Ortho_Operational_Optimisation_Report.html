Why Digemy

Biotekor Ortho’s revenue engine is a 200-strong global field sales force operating across hospitals, theatres and orthopaedic clinics in multiple markets. These reps are not just salespeople — they are clinical partners. The moment a rep walks into theatre, they are representing Biotekor’s credibility, product quality and technical expertise under live surgical conditions. There is no margin for knowledge failure in that environment.

The challenge at Biotekor’s scale is fundamentally a knowledge consistency problem. With 200 reps distributed across international markets, each managing a portfolio of implant systems, consignment stock, surgeon relationships and compliance requirements, the gap between the best-prepared and worst-prepared rep on any given day is significant — and directly costly. That gap shows up as lost cases, slow product adoption, stock reconciliation failures, attrited accounts and credentialing risk.

Digemy is built for exactly this environment. Its reinforced microlearning platform delivers bite-sized, behaviour-changing knowledge directly to reps in the field — before theatre, between calls, at the exact moment preparation matters. The result is a field force that walks into every surgeon interaction with clinical confidence, competitive clarity and operational discipline. Not some of the time. Every time.

“In orthopaedic device sales, the rep who walks into theatre without full clinical command doesn’t just lose the case — they lose the surgeon.”

Digemy Skills Matrix

For Biotekor Ortho, Digemy would build a Global Field Force Competency Matrix that maps every critical knowledge requirement across the rep workforce — from clinical product mastery and theatre protocol, to compliance currency and competitive intelligence. Each competency is scored, tracked and gap-identified at the individual rep level, giving sales leadership a live view of where knowledge risk is concentrated across their global territory network.

The matrix is structured around three dimensions: Clinical Knowledge (implant systems, surgical technique, sizing logic, outcomes evidence), Commercial Execution (competitive positioning, objection handling, portfolio cross-sell, account retention), and Operational Discipline (consignment reconciliation, credentialing compliance, post-market surveillance, post-case reporting). Each dimension is broken into role-specific modules for territory reps, clinical specialists and area managers respectively.

Using the skills matrix as its foundation, Digemy deploys targeted microlearning interventions that close identified gaps before they translate into lost cases, attrited accounts or compliance incidents — turning knowledge management from a once-a-year training event into a continuous, measurable commercial advantage across all 200 reps, in every market.
