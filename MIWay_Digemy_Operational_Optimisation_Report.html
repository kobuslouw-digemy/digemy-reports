Why Digemy

MIWay's entire revenue model runs through people on phones — telesales consultants converting quotes, retention agents defending the in-force book, claims handlers protecting the loss ratio. At the scale of a direct insurer processing thousands of calls daily, knowledge decay is not an occasional risk; it is a structural operational drain that compounds quietly across every shift. Digemy's spaced reinforcement model keeps frontline capability current, compliant, and conversion-ready — without pulling agents off the floor.

“In direct insurance, your product is the conversation. Every fumbled objection, every winnable policy that lapses, every non-compliant disclosure is a training failure dressed up as a business outcome.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy's reinforcement cadences deliver 3–5 minute knowledge bursts tied to MIWay's highest-leakage moments — the telesales pitch, the cancellation call, the claims assessment. With an estimated 2,500–3,500 frontline agents and a ~R5bn GWP base, even marginal improvements in conversion, retention, and claims accuracy recover multiples of any training investment in the first year.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Sales Conversion Decay
Telesales consultants operating with incomplete or outdated product knowledge fail to close winnable quotes. In a direct model where every inbound call is a live selling opportunity, even a marginal drop in close rate compounds across hundreds of agents daily.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5% of GWP
Annual LeakageR50m–R75m
Recovery @10%R5m–R8m
Effects
Declining close rates inflate cost-per-acquisition as unconverted leads accumulate. Consultant confidence erodes over time, creating a self-reinforcing cycle of lower conversion and lower product knowledge engagement.
The Digemy Difference
Weekly product and objection-handling micro-modules — 3–5 minutes, mobile-first — keep telesales consultants sharp on MIWay's value propositions and competitive differentiators at exactly the moment they matter.
Leakage 02
Lapse & Retention Failure
Retention agents who cannot credibly counter price sensitivity, competitive offers, or affordability concerns allow policies to lapse that could have been saved. With each lapse reducing in-force premium immediately, the cost is direct and non-recoverable without expensive reacquisition.
Loss Ratio1%–2% of GWP
Annual LeakageR50m–R100m
Recovery @10%R5m–R10m
Effects
Rising lapse rates erode the in-force book, inflate new-business acquisition costs to offset lost premium, and depress NPS as customers feel underserved at the most critical interaction point in the policy lifecycle.
The Digemy Difference
Retention scripting, competitor counter-argument modules, and affordability restructuring training delivered in reinforced weekly cadences — equipping agents to win the cancellation conversation before it becomes a lost policy.
Leakage 03
Claims Handling Leakage
Claims handlers who misapply policy terms, settle prematurely, or miscalculate entitlements expose MIWay to over-settlement costs and FSCA complaints simultaneously. At the volume of a direct insurer, small per-claim errors aggregate into a measurable loss ratio impact.
Loss Ratio0.6%–1% of claims
Annual LeakageR20m–R30m
Recovery @10%R2m–R3m
Effects
Over-settlement inflates the loss ratio, complaint volumes trigger regulatory scrutiny, and inconsistent assessment quality undermines customer trust in the claims experience — MIWay's highest-stakes brand moment.
The Digemy Difference
Targeted micro-learning on policy wording, claims assessment protocols, and dispute handling — reinforced monthly to stay aligned with product changes, regulatory updates, and lessons from actual complaints data.
Leakage 04
FAIS & Compliance Exposure
In a telesales environment, FAIS compliance is entirely dependent on what agents say on the phone — disclosure obligations, needs analysis scripting, and complaints handling. Gaps here carry both direct regulatory penalty risk and material remediation cost.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1% of GWP
Annual LeakageR25m–R50m
Recovery @10%R2.5m–R5m
Effects
Non-compliant disclosures invite FSCA investigations and remediation payouts. Enforcement risk is amplified by MIWay's high call volumes — a systemic scripting gap affects thousands of interactions before it is identified.
The Digemy Difference
Compliance learning tracks embed disclosure obligations, needs analysis scripting, and complaints handling as non-negotiable reinforced knowledge — keeping every agent audit-ready on every call, every day.
Leakage 05
Cross-sell Capability Gap
MIWay's multi-product portfolio — car, home, life, and business cover — offers significant bundling revenue potential that frontline agents routinely fail to activate through lack of product awareness or absence of structured cross-sell scripting in the flow of a call.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1% of GWP
Annual LeakageR25m–R50m
Recovery @10%R2.5m–R5m
Effects
Single-product policies that could become multi-product bundles represent direct premium uplift foregone — compounded by lower retention rates among unbundled customers who have less financial commitment to the MIWay relationship.
The Digemy Difference
Cross-sell opportunity modules — product suite overviews, bundling scripting, and trigger-moment training — delivered in short reinforced sprints that convert product awareness into habitual call-floor practice.

MIWay's leakage is not a product problem or a pricing problem — it is a knowledge-at-the-moment-of-performance problem.
