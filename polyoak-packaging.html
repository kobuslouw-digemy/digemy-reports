Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Moulding Process & Scrap Losses
Across Polyoak’s blow, injection, compression, and thermoforming lines, machine setters control the process parameters that determine yield, cycle time, and material consumption. Knowledge decay between training sessions is the primary driver of avoidable scrap — and at Polyoak’s production volumes, even a fraction of a percent in excess resin waste compounds into significant annual cost.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageR27.5m–R55.0m
Recovery @10%R2.75m–R5.50m
Effects
Uncontrolled process drift produces off-spec parts that fail at QC, consuming materials that cannot be fully recovered. Resin trial runs to re-establish stable conditions extend downtime and delay production commitments — adding direct cost to every affected run.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific spaced reinforcement modules keep machine setters fluent in the exact process parameters, start-up sequences, and deviation triggers relevant to their assigned lines. Assessed weekly and linked to scrap KPIs, Digemy creates a direct, visible connection between setter knowledge and yield performance.
Leakage 02
Machine Downtime — Maintenance Knowledge Gaps
Moulding equipment is capital-intensive and many fault conditions are operator-recoverable — if the team recognises them early. Breakdowns that escalate to specialist engineers are frequently caused by gaps in preventive maintenance discipline and fault-recognition knowledge at the operator and technician level.
Loss Ratio0.4%–0.8%
Annual LeakageR22.0m–R44.0m
Recovery @10%R2.20m–R4.40m
Effects
Unplanned stoppages delay customer orders, force costly expedited production runs, and impose emergency repair costs that dwarf the price of the knowledge gap that caused them. Repeated downtime from the same fault patterns signals a retention problem, not a technical one.
The Digemy Difference
Machine-specific fault recognition and PM schedule modules reinforced on a spaced cadence ensure technicians retain actionable knowledge between formal maintenance sessions. Manager dashboards flag teams where PM knowledge is trending down before it shows up as a breakdown on the floor.
Leakage 03
Quality & Food Safety Non-Conformances
Polyoak produces primary food, dairy, and beverage packaging under stringent GMP and food contact requirements — supplying blue-chip FMCG customers who enforce strict supplier quality standards. Protocol adherence depends on frontline inspectors and line operators who must apply food safety knowledge correctly on every shift, not just on the day of their last training.
Loss Ratio0.3%–0.6%
Annual LeakageR16.5m–R33.0m
Recovery @10%R1.65m–R3.30m
Effects
Quality failures trigger customer penalties, product holds, and repeat audit findings — each carrying both direct cost and reputational risk with accounts that represent significant revenue. A single non-conformance in a food contact line can cascade into a full production halt.
The Digemy Difference
Daily bite-sized GMP and food safety modules ensure inspectors and line operators maintain certified-level knowledge every day — with manager accountability dashboards that surface individuals or sites trending toward decay before an audit or customer complaint does.
Leakage 04
H&S Incidents & Compliance Cost
Polyoak’s multi-site operations — spanning hot-melt plastics, chemical processing, and high-throughput warehouse environments — create a significant H&S compliance obligation under OHSA. With 2,000+ employees across South Africa, Namibia, and Zimbabwe, knowledge decay in safety protocols is a structural risk that annual refresher sessions cannot adequately address.
Loss RatioMulti-site est.
Annual LeakageR8.0m–R15.0m
Recovery @10%R0.80m–R1.50m
Effects
LTIFR increases drive insurance premium escalation and DoL scrutiny — both imposing costs well beyond the incident itself. Corrective action after an H&S failure is always more expensive than prevention, and always less defensible to the workforce.
The Digemy Difference
Micro-reinforcement of PPE protocols, MSDS handling, machine-specific lockout/tagout, and emergency procedures on a daily cadence drives measurable LTIFR reduction — with site-level dashboards that let H&S managers act before incidents occur, not after.
