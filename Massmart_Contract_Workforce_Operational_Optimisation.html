Why Digemy

Massmart's 20,000–30,000 contract and temp workers are its most operationally exposed population — cycling through Makro, Game, and Builders at scale with minimal structured knowledge transfer. Every undertrained worker on the floor represents dual risk: regulatory exposure under Section 198 of the LRA, and a live conversion gap in high-value categories. Companies like Massmart use Digemy to close that gap at speed, deploying targeted reinforcement learning that is measurable, auditable, and built for high-turnover workforces.

“An untrained temp isn’t a cost-saving measure — they’re a compliance liability and a conversion drain wearing the same name badge.”

Digemy’s spaced reinforcement platform gets contract workers to compliance and category proficiency in days, not weeks — while manager dashboards make knowledge gaps visible before they become CCMA claims or lost sales. Leakage 01
Sales Conversion Gap — High-Consideration Categories
Temp staff placed in electronics, appliances, and general merchandise without product training consistently underperform permanent staff on conversion. On ~R4.9bn of temp-covered floor sales across Makro and Game, a 1–1.5% conversion gap represents material, largely unmeasured revenue loss.

Annual LeakageR49m–R74m
Recovery @10%R4.9m–R7.4m
Effects
Operational teams without structured product knowledge can’t handle objections or close upsell opportunities. Customers leave without converting or purchase at lower basket value — margin loss that compounds across thousands of daily interactions.
The Digemy Difference
Bite-sized, category-specific modules — delivered mobile-first and spaced over the first two weeks — close the product knowledge gap before it costs a sale. Manager dashboards flag readiness gaps by team before temps hit the floor.
Leakage 02
LRA / Section 198 Compliance Exposure
Under the LRA’s deeming provisions, a temp placed for more than three months can be deemed a permanent employee. Without documented, structured compliance training across 20,000–30,000 contract workers, CCMA claims and legal resolution costs escalate rapidly — and a single contested case can trigger group exposure across a shift team.
Dispute Incidence3%–5%
Annual LeakageR15m–R60m
Recovery @10%R1.5m–R6.0m
Effects
Undocumented placement durations and untrained TES providers expose Massmart to deeming claims, CCMA conciliation costs, and potential retrospective benefit obligations. Legal and reputational risk accumulates silently at scale.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy deploys compliance tracks across both client and TES, generating a time-stamped, auditable training record per worker. This demonstrates due diligence to the CCMA and Labour Court — and flags at-risk workers before the three-month threshold is breached.
Leakage 03
Shrinkage — Contract Workforce Contribution
Internal shrinkage in large-format retail runs at approximately 1.5% of sales — roughly R600m for Massmart at this revenue base. Studies consistently show 30–40% of internal shrinkage is attributable to contract and temp staff who lack structured training in receiving protocols, stock-handling procedures, and loss prevention.
Loss Ratio30%–40% internal
Annual LeakageR63m–R84m
Recovery @10%R6.3m–R8.4m
Effects
Poorly trained receiving clerks allow short-deliveries to pass undetected. Inadequate loss-prevention awareness across the warehouse floor enables high-value product misappropriation that often goes unattributed for weeks.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific receiving, stockroom, and loss-prevention modules deployed at onboarding — and reinforced through spaced repetition — create measurable, documented procedure adherence across the full contract workforce from day one.
Leakage 04
Peak Season Productivity Collapse
Black Friday and December festive collectively account for approximately 15% of annual revenue — around R6bn. Massmart absorbs 3,000–5,000 additional temp workers during these windows, who are consistently the least-prepared operational staff at the highest-value trading moments of the year.

Effects
Checkout delays, incorrect product guidance, and floor confusion peak precisely when customer footfall is at its highest — turning the most commercially critical period into the highest-risk service event of the year.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy’s rapid onboarding tracks get seasonal temps floor-ready in under a week, with spaced check-ins during the peak window to sustain retention and surface knowledge decay in real time — before it costs a sale.
Leakage 05
CX Failure & Returns — Electronics & Appliances
Incorrect product advice from undertrained temp staff in high-value categories drives avoidable returns — the most expensive outcome in retail. On Game and Makro’s combined ~R8bn electronics and appliances sub-base, an incremental 0.5–1% return rate attributable to knowledge gaps represents a significant margin hit that largely goes unmeasured.

The Digemy Difference
Product-specific reinforcement for electronics and appliances reduces incorrect advice at point of sale. Return-rate impact is tracked through Digemy’s KPI-linked reporting, giving management a measurable, reviewable ROI signal per category.
