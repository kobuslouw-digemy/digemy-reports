Why Digemy

Verimark runs an exceptional face-to-face training operation — four trainers delivering hands-on sessions to over a thousand sales executives every second month across Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban. But the sharpest gap opens at the worst possible time: the seasonal flexi hiring rush ahead of the festive period, precisely when many retail partners freeze in-store training altogether. Digemy doesn't replace Verimark's trainers or their in-person craft; it gives every rep a mobile, data-light way to recap product knowledge in the gaps between sessions, no store visit required.

“The busiest six weeks of the year arrive with the least-trained hands on the floor — and the stores that need reinforcement most are the ones that ban it.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy's browser-based, data-light recap tool works around retail partners' phone restrictions by living in the quiet minutes before a shift or during a break, not mid-sale on the shop floor. Layered onto Verimark's existing biannual training, it compresses the flexi ramp curve, keeps objection-handling sharp between sessions, and gives the four-person training team live visibility into exactly which knowledge gaps to close next — without adding a single trainer to the road schedule.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Seasonal Flexi Ramp-Up Gap
Verimark brings on a wave of flexi sales staff ahead of the festive season, exactly when its four trainers are stretched thinnest across the country. New hires often reach the floor with only a fraction of the product knowledge veteran reps carry.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2%
Annual LeakageR2.34m–R3.12m
Recovery @10%R234k–R312k
Effects
Flexi reps miss upsell cues and mishandle objections during the highest-traffic weeks of the year. Conversion on peak-season floor traffic runs below what a fully-ramped team would deliver.
The Digemy Difference
New flexi hires get a mobile onboarding recap they can work through before their first shift and revisit in quiet moments on the floor. Ramp time compresses without adding a single trainer to the road schedule.
Leakage 02
New Product Launch Adoption Lag
When Verimark's marketing team briefs a new product line, the training team builds and rolls out the pitch in person, region by region. Retail partners' seasonal training freezes mean some stores don't get the refresh until well after launch.
Loss Ratio20%–35%
Annual LeakageR15.6m–R27.3m
Recovery @10%R1.56m–R2.73m
Effects
Reps in stores caught mid-freeze sell new lines without the pitch, the pros and cons, or the against-competitor positioning the training team built. Early sales momentum on new launches is left on the table in exactly the stores that need it most.
The Digemy Difference
The moment product training is uploaded, every rep can access it the same day — freeze or no freeze — through a low-data browser login that doesn't need a store visit. Launch-week readiness stops depending on trainer travel schedules.
Leakage 03
Cross-Regional Execution Variance
Four trainers cover Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban and the regions between, running groups of thirty to forty reps through identical content week by week. Some regions naturally get more frequent refreshers than others simply based on trainer routing.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR3.9m–R7.8m
Recovery @10%R390k–R780k
Effects
Reps in less-visited regions and outlet types drift further from the current pitch between visits. Customers get a different Verimark experience depending on which store they walk into.
The Digemy Difference
Every rep, regardless of region, gets the same white-labelled content on the same day it's published, closing the gap between what Johannesburg hears this week and what an outlying outlet hears next month.
Leakage 04
Objection-Handling & Retention Decay
Reps are trained thoroughly every second month, but people forget the majority of new information within a day without deliberate reinforcement. Between sessions, sharp objection-handling on specific products fades before the next training touchpoint.
Loss Ratio1%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR3.9m–R5.85m
Recovery @10%R390k–R585k
Effects
A rep who nailed a pitch two months ago may fumble the same objection today. Sales lost to hesitation or a forgotten feature don't show up in any report Verimark currently has.
The Digemy Difference
Bite-size recaps and quizzes between sessions push knowledge into long-term memory instead of letting it decay. A rep who logs in during a quiet ten minutes on shift walks into the next customer conversation sharper than the last.
Leakage 05
Manual Feedback & Knowledge-Gap Blindspot
Today, Verimark hears about a knowledge gap when a rep calls in from a store with a specific problem, or when a regional manager happens to visit. There's no dashboard flagging where accuracy is low before it costs a sale.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR1.95m–R3.9m
Recovery @10%R195k–R390k
Effects
Gaps get found reactively, often after several missed sales, rather than the moment quiz accuracy on a topic starts slipping. The training team can't easily prioritise where a store visit or refresher will do the most good.
The Digemy Difference
Real-time accuracy data by region, store and product line tells the training team exactly where to focus the next in-person session, before it shows up as a lost sale on the floor.
