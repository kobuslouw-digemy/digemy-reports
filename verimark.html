Why Digemy

Verimark's commercial engine runs on frontline performance — demonstrators who convert in-store browsers, call agents who close high-intent DRTV leads, and merchandisers who hold shelf integrity across hundreds of retail partner locations. When any of those touchpoints carries inconsistent product knowledge, sales walk out the door before they're even counted.

Where the loss happens — an estimated R26.3m–R41.6m a year:

Demo Conversion DRTV Call Gap Product Returns Launch Ramp Merch Compliance
“The strongest infomercial in the world can’t close a sale your demonstrator hasn’t prepared for.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy maps and baselines knowledge across every Verimark frontline role — In-Store Demonstrator, DRTV Call Agent, Retail Merchandiser and Field Sales Representative. Spaced-reinforcement microlearning drives retention of product demonstration technique, DRTV call conversion, and merchandising compliance. Real-time dashboards surface knowledge gaps by channel and role, pinpointing exactly where Verimark's R26.3m–R41.6m in annual leakage is concentrating. Evaluation scorecards at individual, banner and network level create accountability — turning that leakage into R2.6m–R4.2m of recovered performance across Verimark's full frontline network.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Demo Conversion Gap
Verimark's retail model depends on demonstrators converting in-store curiosity into sales decisions at the shelf. When demonstrators can't confidently articulate technical specs — IPL wavelength ranges, device safety certifications, warranty terms — customers walk. The sale doesn't recover elsewhere.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.5%
Annual LeakageR1.5m–R3.75m
Recovery @10%R150k–R375k
Effects
Missed in-store conversions, increased browse-and-leave rates, customer complaints about uninformed demonstrators, and damaged retail partner confidence in the Verimark brand at shelf.
The Digemy Difference
Bite-sized product knowledge modules covering technical specs, competitor comparisons, and objection scripts are pushed to demonstrators' phones with spaced repetition — so they arrive at the shelf knowing the product as well as the engineer who designed it.
Leakage 02
DRTV Call Conversion
Inbound DRTV calls represent Verimark's warmest leads — customers who've watched the ad, reached for the phone, and already made a purchase decision in principle. Agents who fumble technical questions, miss upsell windows, or quote incorrect pricing convert those warm leads into hang-ups.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR1.2m–R2.4m
Recovery @10%R120k–R240k
Effects
Abandoned calls, single-unit purchases when bundles were on offer, incorrect orders generating downstream returns, and DRTV media spend that delivers warm leads to underperforming agents.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific call centre modules simulate real objection scenarios with knowledge checks, and spaced repetition ensures agents retain bundle mechanics and product accuracy before their first live call — turning DRTV spend into closed revenue.
Leakage 03
Product Returns & Avoidable Refunds
Verimark's portfolio includes complex devices — IPL hair removal, air fryers, fitness equipment — that generate high returns when customers can't operate them correctly. A significant share of those returns are avoidable with better pre-sale usage explanations, but undertrained demonstrators and agents miss the window.
Loss Ratio25%–35% avoidable
Annual LeakageR6.9m–R9.6m
Recovery @10%R690k–R960k
Effects
Reverse logistics costs, retail partner chargebacks, customer lifetime value erosion, and negative online reviews on high-visibility products that damage brand equity at shelf.
The Digemy Difference
Usage and troubleshooting training modules are embedded in the demonstrator and call agent learning path, ensuring customers leave with both the product and the confidence to use it — dramatically reducing the “doesn't work” return before it hits the shelf.
Leakage 04
New Product Launch Ramp
Verimark's growth model depends on high-momentum product launches. But when demonstrators and call agents are still learning new SKUs after launch day, the critical first-mover window closes before knowledge catches up — permanently surrendering revenue that no amount of catchup training recovers.
Loss Ratio20%–30% shortfall
Annual LeakageR15.0m–R22.5m
Recovery @10%R1.5m–R2.25m
Effects
Slow adoption curves, DRTV media spend wasted on agents who can't close new products, demonstrators defaulting to legacy SKUs, and launch momentum that peaks below potential and never fully recovers.
The Digemy Difference
Verimark can push a fully structured launch campaign into Digemy within 48 hours — ensuring every demonstrator and call agent has completed product training, objection prep, and knowledge checks before the first ad airs. Launch day becomes a genuine commercial event.
Leakage 05
Retail Merchandising Compliance
At hundreds of retail locations across Checkers, Pick n Pay, Makro, Game, and Dis-Chem, Verimark's shelf presence is only as strong as the merchandiser who last visited. Compliance gaps — wrong placement, outdated POS, non-functional demo units — cost sales before the demonstrator even makes contact.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageR1.65m–R3.3m
Recovery @10%R165k–R330k
Effects
Lost shelf share, incorrect pricing visible to shoppers, expired promotional material undermining brand perception, non-functional demo units that actively discourage purchase, and retail partner dissatisfaction.
The Digemy Difference
Visual compliance training modules keep merchandisers audit-ready across all retail banners, with quick-check spaced reinforcement on planogram standards, display protocols, and retailer-specific requirements pushed directly to their mobile devices.

Digemy Skills Matrix
A Verimark skills matrix within Digemy would be structured across four competency tracks: Product Knowledge (technical features, usage, differentiators, and troubleshooting by SKU category), Sales Conversion (objection handling, upsell mechanics, and bundle sequencing by channel), Compliance & Presentation (shelf standards, POS execution, demo unit protocols, and retailer-specific planogram requirements), and Customer Experience (post-sale support, returns prevention, and complaint resolution). Each demonstrator, call agent, and merchandiser receives a personalised learning path based on their channel role and current knowledge score. Launch readiness scores surface to field managers in real time — so no launch window opens with an undertrained frontline, and no product ever reaches a customer through a demonstrator who doesn't yet know it.
