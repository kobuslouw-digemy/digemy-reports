Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Milling Yield & Extraction Loss
Every percentage point of extraction yield lost represents flour that’s paid for in wheat but never sold. At SCC’s throughput scale, protocol deviation at any of the three production units compounds silently across hundreds of thousands of tonnes of annual input.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageFCFA 375m–750m
Recovery @10%FCFA 37.5m–75m
Effects
Suboptimal roller settings and incorrect wheat blend ratios reduce recoverable flour yield — more raw wheat input is required per tonne of output, directly compressing margin on every production run.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces extraction protocols per flour grade (K-Bread, K-Pain Doré, K-Makala) with shift-level dashboards that flag knowledge decay before it becomes yield variance on the production report.
Leakage 02
Quality Rejection & Downgrading
ANOR certification is SCC’s licence to operate at premium pricing. When QC lab technicians or mill operators miss testing checkpoints, batches fail inspection — sold at a discount or written off entirely, eroding revenue per tonne.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageFCFA 300m–900m
Recovery @10%FCFA 30m–90m
Effects
Rejected or downgraded flour batches shrink realised revenue per tonne and damage trust with bakery clients. Repeat incidents risk ANOR certification status, jeopardising SCC’s entire market position.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps QC protocols and batch-approval procedures in active memory through spaced reinforcement, with individual scorecard tracking surfacing which technicians need intervention — before the next batch fails.
Leakage 03
CEMAC Distribution Errors
SCC’s export network spans Chad, CAR, Republic of Congo, Equatorial Guinea and Gabon — each with distinct documentation requirements and logistics complexity. Undertrained dispatchers create costly errors across every corridor.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageFCFA 110m–225m
Recovery @10%FCFA 11m–22.5m
Effects
Incorrect dispatch documentation, wrong SKU loading and poor route knowledge generate claims, returns and strained distributor relationships across SCC’s sub-regional export markets.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy delivers destination-specific training modules for each CEMAC corridor, with dashboard visibility into dispatcher knowledge gaps by export route — turning the sub-region into a knowledge-confident distribution network.
Leakage 04
QHSE & Safety Compliance Failures
A wheat flour milling facility carries significant dust-explosion, food-safety and industrial hygiene risk. Unplanned production stoppages from safety incidents or compliance failures can erase a full production day — and far more in regulatory consequences.
Loss Ratio0.3%–0.7%
Annual LeakageFCFA 250m–530m
Recovery @10%FCFA 25m–53m
Effects
A single unplanned production shutdown costs FCFA 250m+ in lost output. Non-compliance with ANOR food-safety standards can trigger audits, fines or suspension — jeopardising both domestic and sub-regional certification.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces QHSE protocols — dust management, equipment lock-out procedures, hygiene standards — via microlearning tied to maintenance sign-offs, with safety knowledge gaps surfaced on the plant manager’s dashboard before incidents occur.
Leakage 05
Sales & Product Mix Activation
K-Bread, K-Pain Doré and K-Makala serve fundamentally different applications and price points. When reps and distributors don’t understand the distinction, they default to price — undercutting premium SKUs and leaving margin on every sale.
