Why Digemy

Planet Fitness South Africa runs three brands across 51 clubs — with every membership tier, Elevate add-on, and Discovery Vitality integration entirely dependent on frontline staff who are rarely trained beyond induction. When sales consultants can’t close a trial or articulate partner benefit value, when PTs under-programme and under-sell, and when club managers lack the tools to see where knowledge gaps are costing them, the membership base churns faster than it grows. Digemy embeds operational knowledge at club level, creating a measurable skills baseline across every member-facing role that turns frontline staff into the revenue engine, not the leak.

“A free trial your staff can’t close isn’t a member acquisition strategy — it’s an R19,200 gift to a competitor.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy builds a verified knowledge baseline across every member-facing role at Planet Fitness — sales consultants, PTs, group instructors, and club managers — and surfaces gaps in real time through role- and club-level dashboards. Knowledge checks ensure staff can navigate every membership tier, partner benefit, and premium add-on before they face a prospect; scorecard evaluations at individual, club, and regional level track conversion, upsell, and retention outcomes. When Planet Fitness can see exactly where the knowledge gaps are, it can close them before they become churn, cancellations, or missed revenue.

Trial-to-Member Conversion Gap
Planet Fitness offers free trials across all 51 clubs — a powerful acquisition funnel that depends entirely on front desk consultation quality at the close. When staff can’t navigate membership tiers, articulate partner benefit integration, or handle price objections confidently, warm leads walk out and don’t return.

Unresolved objections around tier complexity and partner benefit eligibility push trials toward Virgin Active or budget competitors — eroding CAC efficiency and driving marketing spend without membership growth.
The Digemy Difference
Role-specific modules equip consultants with full tier-comparison knowledge, Vitality and Sanlam Reality benefit mechanics, and structured objection-handling pathways — with spaced reinforcement ensuring this knowledge is retained and applied at every point of sale.
Leakage 02
Preventable Member Churn
SA gym attrition is notoriously high — and at Planet Fitness, the gap between member expectation and experience is most often determined by floor staff quality. Poorly onboarded members, unresolved complaints, and staff who can’t guide training journeys generate early cancellations that erode the recurring revenue base.
Loss Ratio1.0%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR12.0m–R18.0m
Recovery @10%R1.2m–R1.8m
Effects
Cancellations spike in the 3–6 month window when staff fail to transition new members into classes, programmes, and the club community — each lost member representing the full remaining LTV of their contract.
The Digemy Difference
Onboarding and member experience modules ensure floor staff actively guide new members into classes and routines — with club-level retention dashboards that pinpoint exactly where experience gaps are driving the highest cancellation risk.
Leakage 03
Premium Tier & Add-On Upsell Leakage
Elevate, Buddy Tag, and the Black Tag concierge experience represent meaningful incremental revenue per member — but only when staff know the product well enough to spot opportunities and present value with confidence. The gap between what staff know and what the product delivers is where add-on revenue disappears.

Annual LeakageR7.5m–R12.0m
Recovery @10%R750k–R1.2m
Effects
Members leave Planet Fitness without Elevate or Buddy Tag upgrades they would have taken if positioned correctly at natural touchpoints — meaning the group is undermonetising every existing member relationship across 51 clubs.
The Digemy Difference
Product knowledge modules covering every add-on tier and benefit stack — with scenario-based training that gives club staff the language and timing to upsell confidently at the right moment in the member journey.
Leakage 04
Personal Training Revenue Leakage
Personal training is a high-margin revenue stream at Planet Fitness, but one entirely dependent on PTs who can assess, programme, and sell effectively. PTs who underdeliver on results, struggle to communicate progress, or lack the confidence to sell packages leave a revenue base running well below its potential across all clubs.
