Why Digemy

Polyoak Business School has built accredited, technically rigorous training capability across five manufacturing campuses — but classroom delivery without spaced reinforcement means the knowledge trainers invest in decays within weeks of session completion. Without a structured reinforcement loop, trainers have no visibility into who has forgotten what until defects appear on the production floor.
