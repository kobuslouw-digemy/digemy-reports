Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Sales Floor Conversion Loss
Tafelberg floor consultants handle customers across furniture, appliances, and bedding simultaneously — with limited depth on any single range. Without structured product training, the consultation becomes transactional rather than advisory, and high-ticket items walk out the door.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR6.0m–R12.0m
Recovery @10%R0.6m–R1.2m
Effects
Customers who don’t get confident answers go to competitors or buy online. Average basket size shrinks and the consultant’s ability to upsell protective covers, warranties, and accessories is lost entirely.
The Digemy Difference
Spaced microlearning delivers product range training in daily 3-minute modules — building the depth consultants need to guide customers confidently, driving higher conversion and bigger baskets.
Leakage 02
New Seasonal Range Launch Failure
Tafelberg releases seasonal ranges year-round — summer outdoor furniture, winter lounge sets, back-to-school dormitory packages. Without formal launch training, staff sell from familiarity (last season’s stock) rather than actively promoting new arrivals, leaving new range revenue unrealised in the critical first weeks.
Loss Ratio25%–35% shortfall
Annual LeakageR40.0m–R56.0m
Recovery @10%R4.0m–R5.6m
Effects
New range sell-through is slow, seasonal stock ages into markdown territory faster than necessary, and the full margin potential of each launch window is never captured.
The Digemy Difference
Automated launch journeys push range briefings and key selling points to every consultant before the range hits the floor — the team is ready to actively promote new stock from day one.
Leakage 03
Showroom Standards & Visual Merchandising
Tafelberg’s store experience depends on correctly assembled displays, live price tags, and promotional layouts executed per planogram. When floor staff aren’t trained on visual standards and daily audit protocols, showrooms drift out of compliance and the premium feel that justifies price points erodes.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1%
Annual LeakageR4.0m–R8.0m
Recovery @10%R0.4m–R0.8m
Effects
Non-compliant showrooms reduce perceived product value and increase price objections. Promotional displays that aren’t executed correctly mean marketing investment doesn’t translate into sales lift.
The Digemy Difference
Daily visual standards checklists and planogram microlearning ensure every team member knows what “right looks like” before the store opens — with completion tracked for regional oversight.
Leakage 04
Lay-by & Credit Facilitation Loss
A significant portion of Tafelberg’s revenue is facilitated through lay-by agreements and credit products. When consultants misexplain terms, fail to collect correct documentation, or allow accounts to lapse, cancellation rates climb and credit applications are declined unnecessarily.
Loss Ratio1%–2%
Annual LeakageR2.8m–R5.6m
Recovery @10%R0.28m–R0.56m
Effects
Cancelled lay-bys represent lost revenue that is difficult to recover — the customer rarely returns once they’ve cancelled. Compliance failures on credit documentation also create regulatory exposure under the NCA.
The Digemy Difference
Short compliance modules on lay-by administration, NCA terms, and credit process steps ensure consultants handle facilitation correctly every time — protecting revenue and reducing regulatory risk.
Leakage 05
After-Sales Service & Returns Handling
Delivery, assembly, and returns are the most visible moments of the customer journey after the sale. When delivery teams and floor staff aren’t trained on complaint handling, warranty terms, and expectation management, the experience that should build loyalty drives churn instead.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.5%
Annual LeakageR3.0m–R9.0m
Recovery @10%R0.3m–R0.9m
Effects
Poor after-sales experiences translate directly into negative word-of-mouth in tight Western Cape communities. A customer who requests a return is unlikely to repurchase, and brand trust erodes over time.
The Digemy Difference
Scenario-based training on complaint resolution, delivery handoff protocols, and warranty communication ensures every post-purchase interaction reinforces the Tafelberg brand rather than damaging it.

Tafelberg Furnitures’ single largest leakage driver is new seasonal range adoption failure — accounting for up to R56m in unrealised annual revenue as floor consultants default to familiar stock rather than actively selling new arrivals.
