Why Digemy

Planet Fitness is opening five new clubs a year on its way to 90 locations by 2030, which means the tour script, PT upsell and member onboarding that convert in an established Gauteng club need to convert just as reliably in a brand-new Western Cape one. Right now, that consistency depends on how well each new hire happens to be shown the ropes, not on a repeatable system that travels with the brand. Digemy closes that gap by embedding the same standards in every pocket, at every club, from day one.

“A five-club-a-year expansion plan is only as strong as the newest hire’s first week.”

🧠 How Digemy Optimises ROI

Digemy reinforces tour scripts, PT and biokineticist upsell prompts, and new-club onboarding standards through daily, five-minute mobile nudges — no floor time lost, no classroom scheduling, and the same consistency whether a club opened in 1998 or last month.

Top 5 Leakage Areas
Leakage 01
Tour-to-Membership Conversion Gaps
Sales consultants run the same gym tour with wide variance in objection handling, pricing conversations and close technique from club to club. Where tour-to-signup conversion lags even slightly, it compounds every month against a membership base adding roughly 12,000 new members monthly.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageR4.5m–R9.5m
Recovery @10%R0.45m–R0.95m
Effects
Inconsistent closes lose winnable prospects to competitors offering similar pricing with a stronger pitch. Underperforming clubs convert materially fewer walk-ins than top-performing sister clubs running the same national promotions.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces a proven tour-and-close framework through short, repeatable prompts sales consultants can apply between walk-ins, standardising performance across all 51 clubs without a training room.
Leakage 02
Early-Tenure Member Churn
New members who don’t form a training habit in their first 60–90 days are the most likely to cancel, and that habit depends heavily on how well reception, PTs and instructors onboard them in week one. With over 220,000 members and heavy monthly turnover through the funnel, small onboarding gaps translate into meaningful annual churn.
Loss Ratio1.0%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR9m–R19m
Recovery @10%R0.9m–R1.9m
Effects
Members who never get a proper induction or first PT touchpoint disengage quietly and cancel within a few billing cycles. Front-line teams under time pressure often skip the habit-forming steps that keep a new member training past month three.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps onboarding standards top of mind for reception and floor staff through quick, spaced reinforcement, so the induction that keeps a member training happens the same way in every club, every time.
Leakage 03
PT & Biokineticist Attach Gaps
Personal trainers and biokineticists are best positioned to convert members into paid sessions and rehab referrals, but attach rates vary widely depending on how confidently each professional pitches ongoing value. This ancillary revenue is a smaller slice of the business but among the most profitable per member.
Loss Ratio1.5%–2.0%
Annual LeakageR2.5m–R3.5m
Recovery @10%R0.25m–R0.35m
Effects
Members who would benefit from PT support or a biokinetic referral often never receive the pitch, or receive an inconsistent one that undersells the value of ongoing sessions.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy reinforces confident, consistent upsell conversations for PTs and biokineticists, keeping the pitch sharp without adding a formal training session to their week.
Leakage 04
New Club Launch Ramp Shortfall
Each new club opening needs a fully competent team from day one across sales, PT, reception and management, but five launches a year means constantly onboarding new staff to the same standard as established clubs. A slow ramp delays a new club reaching the membership and revenue levels of its sister sites.
Loss Ratio20%–30%
Annual LeakageR13m–R19.5m
Recovery @10%R1.3m–R1.95m
Effects
New clubs frequently take longer than planned to reach mature-club performance, particularly on sales conversion and retention, because new hires pick up the Planet Fitness standard informally rather than systematically.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy gives every new-club hire the same structured, bite-sized onboarding regardless of location or manager, compressing the ramp curve as Planet Fitness scales toward 90 clubs by 2030.
Leakage 05
Compliance & Risk Exposure
Health screening protocols, PT and biokineticist scope-of-practice boundaries, and injury-response procedures protect both members and the brand, but consistency across 51 clubs and 1,000+ staff is hard to guarantee through occasional refreshers alone. Gaps here show up as injury-related complaints, churn and liability exposure rather than a clean line item.
Loss Ratio0.5%–1.0%
Annual LeakageR4.5m–R9.5m
Recovery @10%R0.45m–R0.95m
Effects
Inconsistent screening or scope-of-practice awareness raises injury risk and the reputational and legal exposure that follows, while members who feel unsafe or under-supervised are quick to cancel.
The Digemy Difference
Digemy keeps compliance and safety protocols continuously reinforced across every club and every shift, closing the gap between what’s in the policy manual and what happens on the gym floor.
